On the closed iPhone Duo held on its side, games use the front display's full width, and a setting keeps them clear of the camera instead.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.8-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.8.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.8-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.
## Changed

- **iPhone Duo:** with the Duo closed and held on its side, a game now fills the front display's full width, so the camera covers one corner of the picture. Before, it sat beside the strip down the camera's side.

## Added

- **iPhone Duo:** Game picture in Settings, or Picture in the VNDegen menu, switches between Full width and Clear of the camera, which fits the game below or above the camera, or beside it, whichever shows it larger. Both appear only while the display has a camera strip down one side.

## Known limits

- Clear of the camera needs the game's picture size. Engines drawn through VNDegen's own runtimes and canvas web games such as RPG Maker MV and MZ report it. Godot, Ren'Py, Artemis, KiriKiri, SiglusEngine, Unity and HTML games don't yet, so in that mode they sit beside the camera's strip, as before.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. This was checked on the iPhone Duo simulator with the camera on each side, in both modes, with No Socks RPG (RPG Maker MV) and Kaeru nyo Panyon (System 3.9). The open Duo and the Duo held upright were checked unchanged. The Mac app was built but has no camera strip, so it doesn't change.
- Split View on the Duo, and the cursor pad and on-screen keyboard in the new layouts, weren't checked.
- No code review was done on this release.
