Return from a title menu goes back to the menu in four NekoSDK games, and Mankitsu Happening's Auto runs at the PC's pace.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.32.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.32.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- Funbag Fantasy, Funbag Fantasy 2, Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story and Test Game 94: Return from a menu opened from the title goes back to the title's menu as it was left, with Extra's entries still open if that is how the menu was opened. Before, it showed "Click any mouse button" again (#79).
- Funbag Fantasy 3 if: the title's opening no longer plays again after every Back from a menu (#81).
- Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story: after Previous Choice the message window keeps the scene's last line, with its wait mark and Voice greyed, as on the PC. The first click clears the line and the next takes an option (#80).
- Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story: the title's two lace bands move across, and the side panel's emblem turns while a help is showing.
- Funbag Fantasy 2: at a choice the message window stays bright over the darkened scene, as on the PC.
- Mankitsu Happening: Auto waits on each line as the PC does, measured at its slowest, middle and fastest settings. Before, it used another game's pace.

## Known limits

- In Funbag Fantasy and Test Game 94, Return from a title menu follows what was measured on Funbag Fantasy 2's and Sideboob Story's PC games. Their own PC games weren't checked.
- A menu's own Title button, in a menu opened from the title, lands on the title's first stage. What the PC does there wasn't measured.
- Sideboob Story's lace bands and emblem are close to the PC, not measured exactly, and the disc under Config's three top buttons isn't drawn.
- In Funbag Fantasy 2, a question opened at a choice (the window's Title or Previous Choice) is drawn under the choice, so its Yes and No can't be seen (#82). It was there before this release.
- In Mankitsu Happening, two lines in Japanese quotation marks waited 0.15 to 0.22 seconds longer on the PC than in VNDegen. It isn't known yet whether the PC counts a full-width character as two for Auto.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used.
- These changes were run in the Linux test build against the PC games under Wine. Only the title menu fix was also looked at in the Mac app, in Funbag Fantasy 2. Nothing was run on a simulator.
- Funbag Fantasy 3 if's title was measured in the test build, not compared with its PC game again.
- Each change was read by one reviewer. The small points the last reviewer raised aren't fixed yet.
