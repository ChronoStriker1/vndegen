Wolf RPG Editor games now take the mouse, touch as a mouse, and typed names, and AliceSoft System 3 games play their transitions step by step.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.20.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.20.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: Wolf RPG Editor mouse and text

- Wolf RPG Editor games take the mouse, and touch works as a mouse. Parade Buster and YUME-SAME play their action battles.
- Two Beasts Or Not To Beast! plays from its title to a save, and its saves move to and from the PC.
- Games can type text, on the on-screen keyboard or a hardware keyboard, so MARAGON QUEST takes a name.
- Games can import CSV files.
- The Clumsy Witch Seris gets its minimap and scene text, and its system voices now find their files.

## New: AliceSoft System 3 transitions and small features

- System 3 games play their CE and CD transitions step by step, as on the PC.
- Skip until choice is in Game commands. It passes messages and stops at a choice or a left click.
- The number box works with touch, a keyboard, a controller and the on-screen keyboard.

## Fixes

- Wolf RPG Editor, in every game: NPCs stay where they belong instead of walking at the hero, eight-direction sprites draw correctly, commands that read character and picture details return the right value, and battles that show many pictures no longer slow down.
- Wolf RPG Editor: fonts are found when the Data folder's case differs.
- AliceSoft: Mamatoto's tutorial runs through to its first save point.
- AliceSoft: Rance 4.1 and 4.2 no longer go silent when you pick the FM sound source.
- AliceSoft: in System 4 games, a controller button let go after switching apps no longer releases a key held on the keyboard.

## Known limits

- The Mac app wasn't tested for these features, because the test Macs were locked. The iPad simulator was.
- AliceSoft System 4: the openings can't be skipped the first time a game runs, because the games' own scripts forbid it.
- Wolf RPG Editor: after a save moves between VNDegen and the PC, Two Beasts can differ for a moment. The PC reopens the phone from a VNDegen save, and VNDegen shows the room from either.
