Princess Colette's Great Adventure plays to its ending.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.3-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.3.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.3-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure** plays to its ending: the walls of ice, fire and lightning, the cat's mansion and its fishing where ニャンニャ joins, the 幽玄の塔, the former king's battle, ユグドラシル, the last scene in the throne room and the staff roll. Saves at the cat's mansion, the tower and the throne room after the staff roll were checked both ways with the PC.

## Changed

- **Smile Game Builder:** heroes level up at the PC's experience totals at every level checked, up to 16. Before, the totals above level 10 were up to 3% too high.
- **Smile Game Builder:** rooms under the noon sky show white past their edges, as on the PC, instead of a band of sea, and rooms without sun show black.

## Fixed

- **Smile Game Builder:** with four heroes in a battle, the fourth no longer stands at the screen's edge; the party stands as on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** pressing confirm just as you finish a step now starts the right event. Before, the fire wall by the cat's mansion couldn't be cleared.
- **Smile Game Builder:** walking into the fishing spot by the cat's mansion casts the line, and the first catch is the one the story needs.
- **Smile Game Builder:** opened chests stay open.
- **Smile Game Builder:** large character sprites (コレット in the epilogue, the world map's slimes and zombies) no longer show a second copy above them, and the player no longer stands over the staff roll.

## Known limits

- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure:** the optional dungeons (the cape, the catacombs and the golem cave) and the post-game after the staff roll haven't been played in VNDegen yet.
- **Smile Game Builder battles:** the first two heroes stand about 20 pixels higher than on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the poison balloon shows all the time; the PC shows it now and then. The cat's mansion's books have white spines, and the tower's warp gates have a round shadow the PC doesn't draw.
- **Sacred:** past the Vord forest hasn't been played in VNDegen yet.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
- On iPhone and iPad, tapping a save slot picks the highlighted slot, not the one tapped. The PC's own clicks on that page weren't compared.
- ニャンニャ's level when she joins, against the PC's.
