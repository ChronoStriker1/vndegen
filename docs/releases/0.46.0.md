A new engine: Tmr-Hiro ADV System, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Welcome to the Mizuryuu kei land.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.46.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.46.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.46.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Tmr-Hiro ADV System**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is the English release of Welcome to the Mizuryuu kei land: its title, both routes to their endings, choices, the map, sprites and CGs, voices, music and sound loops.
- The game's own message window, button bars, save, load and auto-save pages, backlog, system screen and EXTRA gallery are drawn from its own parts, so they look as on the PC.
- Tmr-Hiro saves are the PC engine's own files in the game's `save` folder. A save made on a PC loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC. Read text shows in its own colour, and Skip stops at unread text.
- Tmr-Hiro games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/tmrhiro.md](../tmrhiro.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- Tmr-Hiro: only Welcome to the Mizuryuu kei land is known to play. The library's four other Tmr-Hiro games follow in later releases.
- The EXTRA menu's character viewer does nothing.
- The mouse wheel opens the backlog instead of rewinding the scene, as the PC does by default.
- Transitions are crossfades, not the PC's mosaic and wipes, and the map's slide animation is skipped.
- The screen mode and resolution settings do nothing; the app sets the window.
- Without the PC's `savedata.000`, the text speed, window and volume settings last only until the game closes. Read marks and gallery progress are kept either way.
- The controller's Y and Menu buttons do nothing during text, as Escape does nothing there on the PC.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- Both routes were played through to the staff roll in the Linux test build. The Mac app and the iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and a load.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- The scene replay's end button and the music room weren't clicked through. Sound wasn't listened to on the Mac or iPad.
- No code review was done on this release.
