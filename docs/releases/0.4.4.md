Fixes an RPG Maker MZ error screen, "RangeError: length cannot be negative", that could stop No Socks RPG and other MZ games after a long play session.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.4.4-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.4.4.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixed

- RPG Maker MZ games no longer stop with "RangeError: length cannot be negative", which could appear on a warp or any other moment that plays a sound. RPG Maker MZ's own Ogg decoder, which VNDegen uses for Ogg audio on iPhone, iPad and Mac, reuses an ID after 1,000 sounds. If a slowly loading music track still held that ID, the new sound picked up the track's half-finished state and failed. VNDegen now keeps every decoder ID unique. The PC version never hits this, because it plays Ogg without that decoder.

## Known limits

- The fix was checked by reproducing the error on the iPad simulator and the Mac app with 1,100 sound effects after a slowly loading track, not yet by playing on a real iPad.
