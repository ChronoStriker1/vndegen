Two older bugs found by a regression sweep are fixed: KiriKiri games that ship their plugins as `.tpm` files, and PJADV's option settings on quit.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.13-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.13.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.13-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **BISHOP BSX:** Mesu Kyoushi 3 and 4's free-sight meter. On the dress screen, FACE, BUST and HIP pick a fixed view of close-ups, and dragging the knob or clicking the meter sets your own. The view stays on later lines and is kept in saves, so it moves with the PC both ways.

## Fixed

- **KiriKiri:** games that ship their plugins as `.tpm` files, such as Sisters x Lovers, no longer stop at their first line. The plugin now loads the built-in module it stands for (#191).
- **PJADV:** Paradise Cleaning! keeps its Option settings after you quit the game (#192).

## Checked

A regression sweep covered one game per engine after the seven releases from 0.59.5 to 0.59.12, in the Mac app and, for eight engines, on the iPad simulator, along with the library, game pages, Settings, Cheats, Saves and the controller layout editor. It found no regression from those releases. Three older problems it found are open issues: Godot games open web links without asking (#193), Project Hadal's slot screen (#194) and Fallen Kunoichi stopping early in the Mac app (#195).

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator.
- Movie sound was checked from the engines' logs and decoders, not through speakers.
- No code review was done on this release.
