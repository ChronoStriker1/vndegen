BUNNY BLACK 3 is complete: the PC's battle row rule and opening attacks, 戦闘隊列, autosave and popup options, rest scenes and appointment scenes.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.19-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.19.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.19-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.





## Added

- **SystemAoi:** opening attacks before the first round, from 先制暗殺攻撃, 忍び寄る者 and 先制攻撃, on both sides, and 先制攻撃無効化 blocks them.
- **SystemAoi:** 交代 moves a unit to a picked tile, and 戦闘隊列 keeps or restores the board after a battle.
- **SystemAoi:** Option's オートセーブ, with the after-battle and per-step autosaves, and 確認ポップアップ's three levels. Saving over a used slot asks 上書きしますか？.
- **SystemAoi:** leaving 配置 after a change plays the appointment scenes, and resting plays the second rest script's scenes at the PC's rests.

## Changed

- **SystemAoi:** a hit on a row behind the frontmost row its side still holds does half damage, as on the PC, and 防御 adds 10 defence until the unit's next action.
- **SystemAoi:** the 図鑑 and the post holders cut their sprite sheets by the header grid, and in 配置 a held figure is drawn on top at 75% opacity, as on the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** the 学園's cells keep the turn they were built, as in the PC's saves.

## Known limits

- **SystemAoi:** BUNNY BLACK 3 plays from the title to its true ending. What still differs from the PC, or couldn't be measured on it, is listed under Known limits in [docs/systemaoi.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/systemaoi.md). Among them: the wall variants, the PC's random rolls, the snowflakes' sideways drift, the boss's mark on the minimap, and some Option settings (screen mode, cursor and hover descriptions).
- **SystemAoi:** 商会 buying and selling, the turn report, and the late battles, defence battles and final boss weren't compared side by side with the PC.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. BUNNY BLACK 3 was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and compared with the original engine under Wine.
- No code review was done on this release.
