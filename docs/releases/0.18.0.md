Artemis games now play their movies and screen effects, and Rance IX starts and plays its story scenes.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.18.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.18.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: Artemis movies and screen effects

- Artemis games play their movies with sound: studio logos, openings and endings, animated title screens and scene movies. A movie can be skipped with the same keys as on the PC, or a click or tap where the game allows it.
- Long openings stream from the game's files rather than loading whole.
- Screen effects written as shaders, such as blur and mosaic, now show.

## New: Rance IX

- AliceSoft System 4 games built on PartsEngine, such as Rance IX, now start and play their story scenes: logos, the warning, the opening movie, the title, the prologue with its text, and the save and load screens with thumbnails.
- Rance IX's battles and maps are 3D, which VNDegen doesn't run yet. Where the first one starts, at the chapter 1 map, the game stops with a message, and Quit to library returns to the hub. The game page says so before you start.
- Saves made in VNDegen load in VNDegen with the whole screen. Saves also move between VNDegen and the PC game in both directions, and the story carries on, but after such a move the screen shows only the message text and the system bar until the scene redraws.

## Known limits

- Artemis: Ogg Theora movies with Vorbis sound play silent, and Windows Media movies don't play. No test game has either.
- Artemis: in Test Game 78, a save made in the blurred chapel scene stays black after a quick load.
- Rance IX: its 3D scenes don't run. The title's logo animation and other Flat animations don't play, numbers drawn from digit pictures don't show, colour tints on parts aren't drawn, music loops from its start, and message lines wrap a little sooner with VNDegen's fallback fonts.
