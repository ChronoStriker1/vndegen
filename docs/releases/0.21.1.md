Karryn's Prison starts, Artemis quick load and dialogs are fixed, and Godot 3 games with canvas backgrounds draw on iPhone and iPad.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.21.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.21.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- RPG Maker: Karryn's Prison now starts and reaches its first map. Its frame rate and saves haven't been checked yet.
- Artemis: a quick load shows the saved page straight away, instead of staying black until a click.
- Artemis: a game's own dialogs, such as name entry, are now in-app sheets you can use with touch, a keyboard or a controller.
- Godot 3: games with a canvas background, such as Sexual Sealing Dungeon Eronest, now draw it on iPhone and iPad. This was checked on the iPad simulator, up to the game's first map.

## Known limits

- Artemis: on a Mac, a name field selects its text when it regains focus after VNDegen's menu, so the next key replaces the text.
- Artemis: bitmap `.rft` fonts draw only ASCII. Eight pages of Test Game 81 showed no overflow or clipping.
