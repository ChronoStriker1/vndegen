NekoSDK games now run, starting with The Alchemist & His Battle-Scarred Homunculus and Yarinaoshi no Christmas, on VNDegen's own runtime, with saves that move to and from a PC copy of the game.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.24.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.24.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: NekoSDK

- NekoSDK games run on Mac, iPhone, and iPad, on a runtime written for VNDegen, MIT licensed. No other runtime for these games exists. The two games above play from title to ending, with voice, music, choices, saves, the backlog, skip, auto, the gallery, the music screen, and Config.
- A NekoSDK game keeps its menus inside its Windows program, so VNDegen rebuilds them from a table for each game, using the game's own art. Other NekoSDK games are found and say they can't run yet. More come in later releases.
- Saves are the PC game's own files, under `AppData/Roaming` in the game's data folder as on Windows. A PC save loads here, and a save made here loads on the PC. Unlocked pictures and music move with the PC's `cfg.sav`.
- A save made at a choice shows the last line first, and a click then brings the choice, which is what the PC game does with that save. A PC save made at a choice asks it straight away.

## Known limits

- The PC's Config settings don't move with a save: VNDegen keeps its own.
- A save's lock can't be set, and its comment and the "NEW" mark on the latest save aren't there. The bar's Arrange button and the choice of Original or Arrange art aren't there.
- The noise effect some scenes of The Alchemist ask for isn't drawn. A repeating animation saved while it runs plays out once after a load.
- Yarinaoshi no Christmas's Manual and Version info buttons aren't there.
- Menu backgrounds that change on the PC stay the same here. Help pictures on hover and the "Do not show again" box on a confirmation aren't drawn.
- [engines.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/engines.md#nekosdk) has the full list.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The games ran in the Mac app on both test Macs and on an iPad simulator. Simulator timing isn't iPad timing.
- Full playthroughs were run on Linux, on a build just before the last two review rounds' fixes. Those fixes change only how sprites scale and move, and these two games' frames came out the same after them.
- Saves were moved to and from the PC game running under Wine on Linux, not on Windows. Sound was checked by its state, not by listening.
- Text uses a substitute font, so its spacing doesn't quite match the PC.
