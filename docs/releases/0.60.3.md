The library follows the iPhone Duo's design patterns: buttons stay off the fold, the bars move to the side, and grid columns fit the space.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.3-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.3.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.3-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Changed

- **iPhone Duo:** with the Duo half open, nothing you tap sits on the fold. The VNDegen menu, Game commands and Cheats move to the trailing half (or below a fold that runs across), Settings and a game page's buttons keep to the leading half, and the passcode keypad puts its keys below the fold.
- **iPhone Duo:** the library's buttons, Back on a game page and in Settings, and a sheet's Done move into a column on the side where the system puts its bars, below the camera: on the outer display in both orientations, and on the inner display held like a book. Held upright, the inner display keeps the top bar. Buttons that don't fit go into a More menu.
- **Library:** the Grid and Header list take their columns from the space they have, with even counts where the display can fold and a wider gap at the fold. On a 13-inch iPad held upright, the grid shows six columns instead of five.
- **Game page:** the banner's height comes from the page's width, so it's a little shorter on an iPhone held upright.

## Fixed

- **VNDegen menu:** it scrolls when it's taller than the window, so Controller layout is no longer cut off on the closed Duo on its side.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked on the iPhone Duo simulator in every pose, the iPhone and iPad simulators, and the Mac app.
- Split View on the Duo, and the inner display's camera while it's in use, weren't checked.
- No code review was done on this release.
