A second Ikura GDL game, Lightning Warrior Raidy II, and fixes from the new test games.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.57.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.57.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.57.2-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Lightning Warrior Raidy II: Temple of Desire** runs on the Ikura GDL runtime: the opening movie, the town and its shops, the 3D dungeon with its automap, battles, the animated scenes, the backlog, the Extra gallery and the options. Saves are the PC game's own `RAIDY2R.SAV` in the game folder, so they move both ways.
- Import: Construct games packaged for NW.js, such as Dungeon Vixens, and Electron games in electron-builder installers, such as The Titjob Apparition of the Midnight School, now import and run.
- Import: an RPG Maker VX Ace game sold as one self-extracting .exe, such as Succubus Hunt!, now gets a message saying to extract it on a PC or with 7-Zip first, instead of failing.

## Fixes

- KiriKiri: Tenioha! feat. Mami's flashback mosaic transitions play as on the PC, instead of as a crossfade.
- Godot 4: kanji no longer show as empty boxes on the Mac (Life as a Caretaker in Another World).
- Ikura GDL, also in Lightning Warrior Raidy: right-clicking the message window away and back keeps the line, and the save page stays open after saving, as on the PC.

## Known limits

- Raidy II's ending wasn't played through, and only the first dungeon floor was played by hand. Read marks aren't kept, so Skip skips everything. The 3D view's filtering and lighting are close to the PC's, not identical.
- A Construct game for NW.js copied in as a folder, rather than imported, isn't found.
- Only RPG Maker MV and MZ games are unpacked from an Electron app archive; solid NSIS installers aren't covered.
- PC saves of NW.js and Electron games don't carry over, and Dungeon Vixens' Steam achievements do nothing.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Raidy II was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and with every file name's case swapped. Its saves were exchanged with the original under Wine both ways.
- The import changes, the mosaic transitions and the Godot 4 kanji fix were checked in the Mac app only.
- No code review was done on this release.
