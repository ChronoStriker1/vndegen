Adds mouse controls for controllers and fixes two RPG Maker MZ problems seen in No Socks RPG: looping cutscene clips that stuttered or drifted from their sound, and quick presses in timed attacks that didn't register.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.6.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.6.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- Controllers and the touch gamepad can act as a mouse. Any button or stick can move the pointer, left or right click, turn the mouse wheel, or open the on-screen keyboard on iPhone and iPad. "Click" in the mapping editor is now "Left click".
- The mapping editor has four layouts to start from: "Keyboard and mouse", a copy of Steam's template of that name, "Mouse", "RPG Maker controls" and "Xbox gamepad". KiriKiri and TyranoScript games start with "Keyboard and mouse", since neither engine reads a gamepad. Sticks stay sticks in the other layouts.
- On iPhone and iPad, text typed on the on-screen keyboard reaches KiriKiri games, including their pop-up text windows, and web-based games. Pasted text with several lines arrives one line at a time.

## Fixed

- In RPG Maker MZ, short looping movies made from GIFs, such as the Ms. Mittens cutscenes in No Socks RPG, play at their real length. WebKit reads these files as shorter than they are and paused about 300 ms at every loop, so the clips stuttered and fell out of step with their sound. Each loop now matches the length stored in the file to within about 1 ms, including the first pass after a clip changes. Movie frames are also uploaded only when they change, which saves work on every frame. This fix is for RPG Maker MZ only.
- In RPG Maker MZ, quick presses from a controller, the touch gamepad or the keyboard all reach the game. Some games clear their input after each step of a timed attack, and VisuStella's Core Engine then ignored a controller for 24 frames, so the presses that followed were lost. A press and release within one frame was lost too. Both now register. In No Socks RPG's timed attacks, press slightly early: the game closes each hit's window on the exact frame the ring meets the outline.

## Known limits

- Mouse controls were checked on the iPad simulator and the Mac with simulated controller input, not yet with a real controller. Pointer speed, the stick dead zone and the pointer size may need tuning.
- The cutscene fix was checked on all 25 clips and all 9 scenes that show them, on the Mac app and the iPad simulator. The timed attack fix was checked on the iPad simulator and the Mac. Neither has been checked on a real iPad yet.
