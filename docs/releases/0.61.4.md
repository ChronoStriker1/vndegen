## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.4-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.4.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.4-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Changed

- **Sacred (セイクリッド～砕かれた光の冠～)** now plays on Mac, iPhone and iPad through about two thirds of its story: the world map, the port town サンプール and its inn, the voyage, the sand temple, the prison where マオー joins, the snow town マウンティ, the dragon tower where ファフニール joins, シュワントー's tournament and the demon castle, into the depths of the デオズズ volcano. Saves at サンプール, the prison and the volcano were checked both ways with the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** choice windows draw each choice on its own panel, as on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** random encounters bring groups of up to five monsters, as the PC does.
- **Smile Game Builder:** experience totals above level 50, hero stats as they grow, and the skills a hero joining above level 1 already knows now match the PC.

## Fixed

- **Smile Game Builder:** healing skills such as Sacred's ヒール heal; they restored nothing.
- **Smile Game Builder:** saves keep a hero's maximum HP and MP with their equipment, as the PC writes them; a PC save no longer gave a hero the equipment's bonus twice.
- **Smile Game Builder:** Sacred's inns leave the player facing the innkeeper, as on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the edge of Sacred's snow town no longer shows sky below it.

## Known limits

- **Sacred:** from the volcano's fight with six 炎の悪魔 on (the return to ルディアノ, the queen, the last dungeon and the ending) hasn't been played in VNDegen yet. The dragon tower's base, the heaviest scene, can drop below 60 frames a second while other work runs on the Mac.
- **Smile Game Builder saves from 0.61.0 to 0.61.3:** they stored a hero's maximum HP and MP without equipment, so loading one in this version takes a worn item's HP or MP bonus off once. Saves made in this version, and PC saves, are right.
- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure:** the optional dungeons and the post-game haven't been played in VNDegen yet. Its healing skills now restore their share of maximum HP too, which wasn't compared with the PC.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
- The dragon tower's frame rate on a quiet Mac: 58 to 59 fps was measured while other builds ran.
- Whether the PC's save page opens on the newest save; VNDegen opens on slot 1.
