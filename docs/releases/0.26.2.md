Two fixes for The Alchemist & His Battle-Scarred Homunculus.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.26.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.26.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- The Alchemist: the ending follows your choices, as on the PC. Earlier versions gave the happy ending for few happy choices and the normal ending for many. (#58)
- The Alchemist: finishing the prologue is recorded as the PC game records it. A backup moved to a PC now offers Start from Main Story there, and a PC backup's finished prologue counts in VNDegen. A save folder from an earlier version is put right the first time the game starts, changing only that one flag. (#59)

## Known limits

- NekoSDK: scenes unlocked on a PC don't move with a backup yet. The Scene gallery shows those played in VNDegen. This is fixed in work that hasn't shipped yet. (#46)

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The endings were checked by tests on the game's own flow, and the prologue repair by tests and on the PC game under Wine.
