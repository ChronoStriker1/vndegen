Three new engines: YU-RIS on VNDegen's own runtime, and Visual Novel Maker and Twine in the web view.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.38.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.38.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- **YU-RIS**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. No other runtime for YU-RIS exists. The first game is Another Way of Gettin' Paid: its language picker, title, story with text, pictures, music and voice, backlog, Config, saving and loading. The games' menus are their own scripts, so they look and work as on the PC.
- YU-RIS saves are the PC engine's own `.sd` files in the game's save folder. A save made on a PC loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- YU-RIS games run at their own speed on 60 Hz and 120 Hz displays.
- **Visual Novel Maker**, for NW.js and Electron builds, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Saves are the PC's `.vndata` files beside `Game.exe`, so Ludusavi backups move between VNDegen and a PC.
- **Twine 1 and 2**, with Harlowe, SugarCube and the other story formats: a loose HTML file, an NW.js or Electron wrapper, or a Unity player that shows the story through Zen Fulcrum's Embedded Browser. Twine pages scroll by touch and with LB and RB.
- Controls. YU-RIS games start on "Visual novel". Visual Novel Maker games start on "Visual novel" with A as a left click, shown as "Visual novel for VN Maker". Twine games start on "Mouse", where A clicks the link under the pointer.
- Visual Novel Maker games can drop the NW.js or Electron files they don't need in VNDegen, as TyranoScript games can.
- [docs/yuris.md](../yuris.md) and [docs/htmlvn.md](../htmlvn.md) have the details.

## Fixes

- A page that scrolls now stays scrolled. The NW.js layer used to send every page back to its top on each scroll, which only showed once Twine brought pages longer than the screen.

## Known limits

- YU-RIS: only Another Way of Gettin' Paid and one Japanese game on the oldest engine version are known to play. MA☆KO HUNTER reaches its title and shows English text, but its menus show in Japanese and it isn't finished. Saimin Seishidou -Secret Lesson- reaches its title and isn't finished; its story is in English and its title and menu buttons are Japanese pictures, as on the PC. Saimin Gakushū: Secret Desire stops as it starts. A YU-RIS game with no `yscfg.dat` in its folder isn't recognised. These are being worked on.
- YU-RIS: YMV and Flash movies, MIDI, CD audio, E-mote, picture masks and a few picture effects aren't built, and a sound's pitch setting isn't applied.
- Visual Novel Maker: Siren's Call reads on only with a click, by the game's own script. Return and Control do nothing in its dialogue, so X doesn't skip. This wasn't compared with the PC.
- Visual Novel Maker: an Electron game that keeps its data in AppData stops at its first save, and one set to internal storage can't save. No known game does either.
- Twine: saves stay in VNDegen and don't reach a PC. SugarCube's "Save to disk" and "Load from disk" don't work. Pictures a story loads from the web don't show.
- Violated Hero was seen to stop partway through its opening (#85). It hasn't been reproduced since and is still open.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or hardware controller was used.
- The Mac mini's screen was locked for all of this work. In the Mac app every check was made through logs, the app's test hooks and, for YU-RIS, captures of the app's own window. Nobody looked at the Visual Novel Maker and Twine games in the Mac app with their own eyes; they were looked at on the iPad simulator.
- YU-RIS: two games were played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and the iPad simulator, and their saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine. The 120 Hz pacing was checked in the Linux test build only, not on a 120 Hz screen. Frame rate wasn't measured, since neither game has movies.
- Visual Novel Maker: one game, Siren's Call. Its movie was measured at 30 fps on the Mac app and the iPad simulator. An NW.js build was covered by unit tests only.
- Twine: three games, one of them in a Unity player. One game's own interface has no save button, so its save and load were driven through SugarCube's dialog from a test script.
- The TyranoScript change that shares its browser save rule with Twine was covered by unit tests, not by a game in the app.
- Removing a Visual Novel Maker game's engine files was counted on its folder, not carried out in the app.
- Not looked at on an iPhone.
- No code review was done on this release.
