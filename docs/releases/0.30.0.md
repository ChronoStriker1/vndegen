Yabai! -Fukushuu Yami Site- plays, from New Game to all three endings, with saves that move to and from a PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.30.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.30.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- NekoSDK: Yabai! -Fukushuu Yami Site- plays. It's the fourth game that keeps its story order in the program itself, so VNDegen plays it from a table of that order traced from the PC game: the prologue, five truths, the punishments and openings between them, the time-out and wrong-vote game overs, and the three endings.
- The interrogation hub: its drifting question bubbles, the hours each question costs, and the stages that unlock as on the PC.
- The confession tree, with its stamps, its red lines between right answers, and a replay of any question already asked.
- The vote at the end of each truth: the pick screen, then the vote with its countdown.
- Viewer comments that cross the picture during scenes, and the Comment button that turns them off.
- The message-board roll after each ending's first chapter.
- Its own menus: the title, Config, the CG gallery with its viewer panel and slideshow, the Scene gallery that replays chapters, the music screen, and the questions the PC asks before a load, a save, an overwrite, the title and exit.
- The flickering bulb: a background or standing picture that has a dark version flickers between the two, from the game's own pattern file. Config's 背景の電球演出 turns it off.
- A PC save loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC, at the hub, in a question, at a pick and in the story.
- Where the window is nearer 4:3 than 16:9, as on an iPad, the picture stays on top and the message window moves under it.
- Fonts: games that ask for Calibri now get Carlito, which has Calibri's widths, so lines break where the PC's do.

## Fixes

- NekoSDK, the games with GOOD Timing: Extend stays as you left it, between scenes and after closing the game, as on the PC. Before, it switched itself off at every new scene. Switching GOOD Timing off now ends an extension at its next script.
- NekoSDK, the games played from a story table (Funbag Fantasy, Funbag Fantasy 2 and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story 2): a save keeps its place in the story when a later VNDegen changes the game's table.
- NekoSDK: a message window that slides into place stopped a few rows short until something else redrew the screen.

## Known limits

- Yabai!'s hub doesn't draw the pink thought lines that drift behind the bubbles, and its bubbles always start from the same places. Both are being worked on.
- The bulb doesn't flicker on standing pictures 000 to 009 or on five of the backgrounds, where it isn't known which picture the PC pairs with which.
- A settings file VNDegen wrote turns the bulb setting off when the save folder goes back to a PC. Turn it back on in the PC's Config.
- The endings' roll was timed on the third ending. The first two use the same timing.
- In the vote, the raised hands' arrangement, how a bubble pops in, and how long 投票終了 shows are drawn as best fitted, not confirmed against the PC.
- Config's tutorial setting and text speed weren't checked against the PC.
- Two NekoSDK games are still being built and say on their page that they can't run yet: Mankitsu Happening and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Yabai! was played headless on seven routes (the three endings, wrong votes at truths 1 and 4, time-outs at truths 1 and 5), in the Mac app through a vote and the third ending, and on the iPad simulator through the third ending.
- Saves moving to and from a PC were checked with the PC game running under Wine on Linux, not on Windows.
- The Mac app was checked on one Mac only.
- A standing picture's flicker was compared with the PC on one pose.
- The last small fixes from review, to Yabai!'s saves and to the bulb, were tested but not read by a reviewer.
