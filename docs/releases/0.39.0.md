Three more YU-RIS games: MA☆KO HUNTER, Saimin Seishidou -Secret Lesson- and Tales of the Yokai Incident Inspectors.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.39.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.39.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- **MA☆KO HUNTER** plays: its title, the story in English, the map, the opening movie with its sound, backlog, saving and loading.
- **Saimin Seishidou -Secret Lesson-** plays: its title, the story in English, its brand movie, backlog, saving and loading.
- **Tales of the Yokai Incident Inspectors** plays, in Japanese: its title, story, choices, backlog, saving and loading.
- Saves of these games move to and from a PC, as for the first YU-RIS games.
- A YU-RIS game with no `yscfg.dat` in its folder is now recognised by its `ysbin.ypf`.
- [docs/yuris.md](../yuris.md) has the details.

## Fixes

- YU-RIS: a save made in VNDegen by a game on save format 312, such as MA☆KO HUNTER, now loads in the PC engine. It was refused as incompatible.
- YU-RIS: a movie no longer sends the same frame to the screen again on every display refresh.
- YU-RIS: loading a save that holds a script position the runtime can't read no longer reads freed memory when it logs the problem. No tested game makes such a save.

## Known limits

- MA☆KO HUNTER's and Saimin Seishidou's title and menu buttons are Japanese pictures, as on the PC. Their stories are in English.
- Saimin Gakushū: Secret Desire still stops as it starts. Its engine version reads its story in a way the runtime doesn't have yet.
- Two library games on the oldest engine version (406), one whose text format changed (494) and one that looks like an unpacked copy haven't been tried.
- YU-RIS: YMV and Flash movies, MIDI, CD audio, E-mote, picture masks and a few picture effects aren't built, and a sound's pitch setting isn't applied.
- Violated Hero was seen to stop partway through its opening (#85). It hasn't been reproduced since and is still open.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or hardware controller was used.
- These three games weren't run in the Mac app. The Mac mini's display was asleep and locked. They were played in the Linux test build and on the iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and what changed is in code both platforms share.
- Movie frame rate was measured on the iPad simulator only: MA☆KO HUNTER's opening at 29.97 frames a second against 29.97, and Saimin Seishidou's brand movie at 30.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engines under Wine in both directions for save formats 312 and 480. Format 466 was checked for 0.38.0.
- Not looked at on an iPhone.
- No code review was done on this release.
