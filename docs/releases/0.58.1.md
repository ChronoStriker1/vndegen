Fixes for President Yukino on Wolf RPG and Yu Crossing Animals on Godot 3, from the checks in #190.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.58.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.58.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.58.1-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **Wolf RPG:** President Yukino now plays past its prologue, opens its menu and shows its portraits, and its saves load in the PC version. Five differences from the PC engine were fixed:
  - a common event called by name runs the last event with that name;
  - "move to registered position" works;
  - "×=" with a division multiplies first;
  - an event started by a parallel event runs on the next frame;
  - Wolf 2.2x games get saves the PC version can load.
- **Godot 3:** Yu Crossing Animals' hero can walk. Godot 3.5 and later find the nearest point on the navigation map with too little precision; VNDegen now uses full precision there.
- **YU-RIS:** a missing optional button image is logged once instead of every frame.

Two things in #190 needed no change: Yu Crossing's language list starts on Polski on the PC too, and President Yukino's calls to common events it doesn't have are skipped on the PC too.

## Known limits

- After loading a President Yukino save, its day bar and portrait come back only once the menu has been opened. The PC engine restores pictures from the save; VNDegen doesn't yet.
- Drain Dungeon and Journey of Depravity, before they have saves of their own, now get the same older save format. It wasn't checked in their PC versions.

## What wasn't tested

- Yu Crossing Animals' save, which the game allows only far into the story.
- The Wolf RPG changes on the iPad simulator. They were checked in the Mac app and the Linux test build.
- No code review was done on this release.
