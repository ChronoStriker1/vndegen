The Cheats page now loads in RPG Maker games that hold fractional gold, such as No Socks RPG on its Too Easy difficulty.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.22.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.22.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- RPG Maker MV and MZ: Cheats failed with "Couldn't read the game" once a difficulty plugin had awarded fractional gold. The page now shows the balance rounded, and the game and its saves keep the exact amount. For example, a game holding 38.4 gold shows 38, and adding 1,000 shows 1,038 and leaves 1,038.4 in the game.
- Cheats: the loading spinner stops when the page can't read the game, and the error clears when you reopen the page.

## What wasn't tested

- Checks ran on an iPhone simulator with a No Socks RPG save made by playing on Too Easy. The original report's save from a real iPhone wasn't available, and no physical device was used.
- No other game with fractional gold was tried, and the Mac app wasn't run with this fix. It uses the same code.
