A new engine: Princess Colette's Great Adventure plays on Smile Game Builder, VNDegen's own runtime for it, from the title to its world map and battles.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **Smile Game Builder:** a new engine, on a runtime written for VNDegen. Princess Colette's Great Adventure plays on Mac, iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad, from the title through the castle, the dungeon scenes where Colette joins you and the castle town, out onto the world map with its wandering monsters and battles. The game is in Japanese, as on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder saves** are the PC version's own files in the game's `savedata` folder, so they move both ways with a PC copy and Ludusavi. Save from the game's menu or Game commands, and load from the title's Continue or Game commands.
- **Smile Game Builder controls** start on the RPG Maker layout: A confirms, B cancels, Y or Menu opens the game's menu, X dashes. On a touch screen a tap confirms, and holding a finger on the map opens the menu, as the PC's mouse does.

## Fixed

- **Ikura GDL:** a name a script passes to a command no longer shares one buffer with the next name, which now and then changed what a Lightning Warrior Raidy II save held (#204).

## Known limits

- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure** past its first world-map area hasn't been played through yet.
- **Smile Game Builder battles:** effects are simpler than on the PC and the camera doesn't swing in on the acting fighter. Critical hits, skill damage and escaping are close estimates of the PC's rules.
- **Smile Game Builder touch:** letting go after holding on the map doesn't also pick the menu's first item, as it does on the PC.
- **Sacred** (Smile Game Builder) is recognised and starts but isn't supported yet.
- **iPad simulator:** with the touch gamepad shown, Princess Colette runs at 34 to 50 fps, because the simulator is slow to draw the gamepad's see-through buttons over the game. It runs at 60 fps with the gamepad hidden, and on the Mac. A real iPad hasn't been measured.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
- The iPhone's castle town scene, and Sacred past its first map.
- Whether the PC also lets townspeople stand in door cells, as VNDegen sometimes does at the castle door and the town's pier. Stepping back clears it.
