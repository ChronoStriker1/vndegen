A third BISHOP BSX game: Mesu Kyoushi 4 ~Kegasareta Kyoudan~.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.55.4-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.55.4.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.55.4-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- Mesu Kyoushi 4 ~Kegasareta Kyoudan~, in its English machine translation, runs on the BSX runtime: the opening and staff-roll movies, the school map, events and choices, lines voiced by two characters and looping voices, blinking faces and bust-ups tinted to the scene, the FACE, BUST and HIP views, the backlog and settings.
- Save and Load use the PC's own files, so saves move between VNDegen and a PC copy both ways, including the map's auto-save.

## Known limits

- The museum isn't there yet.
- The STRIP button (the dress screen) does nothing.
- The settings screen lacks BGM SELECT, the per-character voice details and the BGV volume.
- The save slots show the weekday without the day number.
- A space the translation puts after 「 is drawn; the PC drops it.
- Settings are VNDegen's own, not the PC's.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used, and no iPhone simulator was used for this game. A play reached an ending, the staff roll and the title in the Linux test build. The Mac app and an iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and loading it after a restart, and the iPad also with every file name's case swapped.
- The opening movie showed all of its frames on the Mac and the iPad simulator.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
