False Myth 2 plays: VNDegen reads Wolf RPG Editor 3.31 and later archives. BUNNY BLACK 3's dungeon walls use the original's texture variants.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.6-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.6.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.6-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **Wolf RPG Editor:** archives from Wolf RPG Editor 3.31 to at least 3.343 (crypt version 0x14B) open, so games made with those versions start. False Myth 2 plays from its prologue and first battle through the title, the config, the Main Gate scenes and the Sanctuary, with saving from its menu and loading from its title. Each part was compared with the game on the PC.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** `<SCREENSHOT>` pictures, mirrored pictures (a negative zoom), the `\wE` outline width, and character images ending in `T.png` and `TX.png`, with their standing frames.

## Fixed

- **BUNNY BLACK 3:** dungeon walls now use the same texture variants as the original, wall for wall.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** the picture flash effect fades back as on the PC, instead of leaving the picture tinted.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** an erase or move over a wide range of picture numbers reaches every picture in it.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** a condition runs its else case whenever it has one.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** an event waiting for a move route goes on once that route ends, even if another route has started since.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** the BGM, BGS and SE volume settings (system variables 100 to 102) start at 100% and set the volume.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** the choice window's position (system variable 3), the pause icon's (5 and 6), the party command's gap-closing and transparency, and `\A+` and `\A-` in text work as the manual describes.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** pictures that are fully transparent are no longer decoded each frame, which made False Myth 2's battles run at about 2 fps.

## Known limits

- **False Myth 2:** past the first dungeon's tutorial it hasn't been played in VNDegen. The blur behind its tutorial panels, the X button image in the battle's "Hold: End Turn" label and the diamond hint beside the hero (a square in VNDegen) aren't drawn as on the PC, and its choice box is a little wider. In the tutorial battle, the "Hold X key: Skip" label stays over "Hold: End Turn".
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** archives of crypt versions after 0x14B (Wolf RPG Editor 3.5 and later archives that encrypt their header) still don't open.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
- False Myth 2's dungeons, later story and other battles.
