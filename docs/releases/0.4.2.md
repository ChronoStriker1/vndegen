Controllers and the touch gamepad now act as an Xbox controller in games, and touch gamepad buttons press when you touch them. Also fixes a blur over the lower part of No Socks RPG's map on iPad.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.4.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.4.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- In a game, every button from the touch gamepad or a real controller reaches the game as the same Xbox button, and the game reads it with its own gamepad support. RPG Maker MV and MZ read it natively. A TyranoScript game that turns on its gamepad gets the buttons its KeyConfig.js binds, and VNDegen's TyranoScript actions for the rest.
- The button mapping editor lists every Xbox button, in Gamepad, Keyboard, Mouse and Nothing sections. Keyboard keys appear only under Keyboard.
- At the top of the editor, "RPG Maker controls" maps the buttons to RPG Maker's keys, and "Reset to gamepad defaults" clears every remap.
- The touch gamepad adds LT, RT and View, and its D-pad is one cross-shaped pad.

## Fixed

- Touch gamepad buttons press when your finger touches them, not when it lifts. While a game runs, VNDegen also asks iOS to let the game have touches at the screen edges first.
- On iPad, No Socks RPG's map no longer blurs below the middle of the screen after the game has been in portrait. Its tilt-shift effect read the window size once, so it now gets the game's size.

## Changed

- Remaps saved by earlier versions convert once to what they sent before, so remapped games play the same. For example, Confirm in an RPG Maker game becomes the Return key.

## Known limits

- The touch press timing and the edge-touch change were tested on the iPhone and iPad simulators, which don't hold back edge touches the way a device does. A real device may still delay presses right at the screen edges.
- A real controller on Mac has not been tested with the new Xbox defaults.
- The small menu button in the corner still opens the VNDegen menu when you lift your finger, like other buttons in iOS.
- Save sync still connects over SSH in this release. Connecting to an SMB share is in progress.
