A new engine: Eushully AGE, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Kamidori Alchemy Meister. This is a first release: it was played through the Prologue chapter.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.57.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.57.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.57.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Eushully AGE**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is Kamidori Alchemy Meister, with the English patch: the opening movie, the title, the story and town events with their choices, the battle maps with their cut-in movies and sound, crafting, shops, the party and mission screens, the settings, the Back Log and the BGM room.
- AGE saves are the PC engine's own files, so a save made on a PC loads in VNDegen and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- The movie player now plays MPEG-1 Layer I sound, which Kamidori's battle cut-ins use.
- [docs/age.md](../age.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- Only the Prologue chapter was played: the story, the tutorial map and Yuidora Woodland to stage clear, one craft and one purchase. Later chapters and their maps, more skills and allies, the Arena's fights, the mine, Enhance, Remodel, selling and the endings weren't reached. They use the same systems, but a long play may still find a bug there.
- The PC's menu bar and its dialogs (version, settings, exit) aren't there; the game's own menus are.
- Text uses VNDegen's substitute fonts, so letter shapes and spacing differ slightly from the PC's.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- Three random runs of about 18,000 clicks and keys went through the same screens and found two bugs, both fixed. They didn't get further into the game than the hand play.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
