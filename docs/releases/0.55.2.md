A second BISHOP BSX game: Mesu Kyoushi 2 ~Injuu no Rasen~ Immoral Edition.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.55.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.55.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.55.2-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- Mesu Kyoushi 2 ~Injuu no Rasen~ Immoral Edition, in its English translation, runs on the BSX runtime: the story, events with faces and overlays, the map, choices, STRIP and VIEW, the backlog, settings, and Save and Load. Its saves move between VNDegen and a PC copy both ways.

## Known limits

- The museum isn't there yet; its title button does nothing.
- Settings are VNDegen's own, not the PC's.
- Cut-ins and the voice repeat modes aren't there.
- VIEW uses three fixed positions, without the free-sight meter.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used, and no iPhone simulator was used for this game. A skip-play reached an ending in the Linux test build. The Mac app and an iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and loading it after a restart, and the iPad also with every file name's case swapped.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
