Mankitsu Happening plays, from New Game to every ending, with its bonus stories and its School Swimsuit DLC, and saves that move to and from a PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.31.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.31.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- NekoSDK: Mankitsu Happening plays. It's the fifth game that keeps its story order in the program itself, so VNDegen plays it from a table of that order traced from the PC game: the opening, five weeks of two halves, the three heroines' routes and the shared ending that opens once all three are finished.
- The select screens: four cards each half-week, chosen by each heroine's hearts as on the PC. A heroine at four hearts by the last pick ends in her route.
- The face pictures beside the text, with their moves and animations, and the bars that wipe the screen between scenes.
- Its message window has no plate. The lower row of buttons shows when the pointer is near the bottom, over the red liquid.
- Its own menus: the title, Save and Load, Config with Individual Voices, the CG, Scene and Music screens, and the backlog.
- Config's Questionnaire Privilege switch dresses the heroines' standing pictures and faces as the PC does, and re-dresses a picture on screen as the switch is clicked.
- The bonus stories: when the release's second archive is in the game's folder, the title adds three book covers, each playing one bonus story, and an Information box.
- The School Swimsuit DLC is a game of its own. Add its folder to the library like any other game.
- A PC save loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC, at a select screen, at a pick, in a route and in the shared ending.

## Fixes

- NekoSDK: a Scene replay that reaches its end goes back to the Scene screen. Before, it ended at the title (#73). Funbag Fantasy 2 comes back on the tab and page its card was on, as on the PC. Funbag Fantasy and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story 2 come back to the Scene screen as it opens, and Sideboob Story 2's replay started from the CG viewer comes back to the viewer.
- NekoSDK, in Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi, Funbag Fantasy 2 and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story 2: with a picture open in the CG viewer, a click or tap goes to the next picture wherever it lands. Before, one that landed over a gallery button hidden under the picture pressed that button, and the picture stayed on screen (#75).
- In the same three games, the right button on an open picture closes the picture and stays in the gallery. Before, it left the gallery for the title.

## Known limits

- A Scene replay started from a menu inside the story loses the story: leaving the Scene screen afterwards lands on the title (#74). Save before starting a replay from inside the story. A fix is being reviewed and comes in the next release.
- Mankitsu Happening's text speed and auto speed use another game's curves, not yet measured on its own PC.
- Two of its scene wipes carry a caption picture on the PC that VNDegen doesn't draw yet, and the wipes' timing is approximate.
- The liquid's bubbles, the title's rising dots, where the moving trims and the Information text start, and one face shake are drawn in the PC's style, not measured.
- In its CG viewer, a click past an entry's last picture closes the picture. On the PC it goes on into the next entry's pictures.
- Yabai!'s limits from 0.30.0 still stand.
- One NekoSDK game is still being built and says on its page that it can't run yet: Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Mankitsu Happening was played headless to every ending, in the Mac app through a route's end and the School Swimsuit DLC from start to end, and on the iPad simulator through a route, the shared ending and the School Swimsuit DLC. The three bonus stories on the title were played headless only.
- After the CG viewer fix, the iPad simulator check was one tap test in Mankitsu Happening. The other three games with that viewer were covered by the automated test, not played.
- Funbag Fantasy's and Sideboob Story 2's Scene replays weren't compared with their PC games for which tab they return to.
- Saves moving to and from a PC were checked with the PC game running under Wine on Linux, not on Windows.
- The Mac app was checked on one Mac only.
- The last small fixes from review were tested but not read by a reviewer.
