The museum for all four Mesu Kyoushi games on BISHOP BSX, and a fix for Mesu Kyoushi 4's choices.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.57.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.57.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.57.1-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **The BSX museum**, from the title screen, in all four Mesu Kyoushi games: the music room with its volume bar, the CG gallery and its viewer, and scene replay, played straight away or through one of ten playlists.
- In Mesu Kyoushi 3 and 4, the background voice room plays each character's loop voices, with a volume for each character and one for all.
- The museum reads and writes the PC's own `common.dat`, so CGs seen, scenes opened and playlists are shared with the PC version. Mesu Kyoushi 3's two rate achievements unlock as they do on the PC.

## Fixes

- Mesu Kyoushi 4: a choice now sets its value and carries on, as on the PC. Since 0.55.4, every choice took the same branch.
- BSX: CG layers with a tag in their name, such as cut-ins and breath, are drawn and marked seen, so Mesu Kyoushi 4's CG rate can reach 100.
- BSX: loop voices play on their own channel at the settings' level, instead of sharing one with a line's second voice.

## Known limits

- The bust-up viewer (立ち絵鑑賞) isn't there yet; its button is shown as off.
- Mesu Kyoushi 4's viewer button that turns off the ahegao faces is shown but does nothing.
- The background voice room's per-character volumes reset when the app starts.
- VNDegen doesn't create `common.dat`. A game copied without it keeps no read marks, achievements or museum records.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The museum was checked in the Linux test build for all four games, in the Mac app with Mesu Kyoushi 3 and 4, and in an iPad simulator by touch with Mesu Kyoushi 3, with every file name's case swapped.
- Its screens were compared with the PC version under Wine.
- No code review was done on this release.
