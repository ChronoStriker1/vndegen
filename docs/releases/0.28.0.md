Funbag Fantasy and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story 2 now play, so eleven NekoSDK games run. Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi gets its 4:3 mode, which fills an iPad's screen with the text below the picture.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.28.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.28.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: two more NekoSDK games

- Funbag Fantasy and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story 2 play from the title to every ending. They're the first of six games that keep their story order inside the PC program, so VNDegen follows a story table traced from the PC game.
- Their title, save, load, Config, gallery, music, log and dialog screens are rebuilt from the PC game and measured against it.
- Saves move both ways with a PC copy of each game, including Funbag Fantasy's Previous Choice files, so Return to Choice works across the two.
- Each ending's staff roll, text speed, auto speed and auto wait follow the PC's.
- Sideboob Story 2's character gallery works as on the PC.
- Funbag Fantasy's story text uses DejaVu Sans, chosen as the closest match to the Verdana its PC uses.

## Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi

- Its 4:3 mode works. The scene keeps its 16:9 picture on top, and the text box moves down into a 240-pixel strip below it, as on the PC. VNDegen picks 4:3 when the game's space is squarish, such as an iPad or a squarish Mac window, and 16:9 when it's wide, switching between frames without reloading the scene. Choosing a screen size in the game's own Config fixes that shape; choosing it again goes back to automatic.
- Its Config draws the dark band along the bottom that the PC draws.

## Fixes

- Horny Elves and a Moral Orc: its gallery cards moved one pixel right, to where the PC draws them.

## Known limits

- Funbag Fantasy's story text is in a stand-in for Verdana, and its Config numbers are fitted to the font Wine uses.
- The Alchemist's gallery card frames may sit a pixel off, as no clean PC screenshot could be made to check them.
- Two parts of GOOD Timing's rules are still worked out rather than watched on a PC: which group of text plays next, and when Extend resets.
- Four NekoSDK games are still being built and say on their page that they can't run yet: Mankitsu Happening, Yabai! -Fukushuu Yami Site-, Funbag Fantasy 2 and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Funbag Fantasy was played to one ending in the Mac app and another on the iPad simulator, and to all five without the app. Sideboob Story 2 was played to both endings in the Mac app, and through its title, saves and a choice on the iPad simulator.
- The nine NekoSDK games that played before were each checked again in the Mac app: a choice where the game has one, a save, a load and a gallery replay.
- Saves moving to a PC were checked with the PC games running under Wine on Linux, not on Windows.
- The Mac app was checked on one Mac only.
