BUNNY BLACK 3's title menus, battle backdrops, enemy groups that move as on the PC, and saves that match the PC's field for field.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.11-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.11.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.11-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.



## Added

- **SystemAoi:** the title's Load reopens on the last page used, and the Option screen works as on the PC: window transparency, volumes, text speed and click settings take effect and are kept.
- **SystemAoi:** the Extra menu, with CG閲覧, シーン回想, エクストラシナリオ, 音楽鑑賞 (64 tracks), クイズ and ヒント.
- **SystemAoi:** save comments, readable and writable both ways with the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** battles show the dungeon behind the board, the panels use the original's digits, and values turn red at a quarter of their maximum, as on the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** enemy groups start where they do on the PC and move along the maps' routes. Replays of PC autosaves matched in every check.
- **SystemAoi:** the purple passage markers, and the walking arrows on the 3D view, which appear on hover and walk on a click or a tap. The touch pad's corner buttons sidestep.
- **SystemAoi:** the 迷宮's breakthrough shows the facility bursts.

## Fixed

- **SystemAoi:** saves now carry the script cache and the ability numbers, and six fields that were wrong are fixed, among them the element order, swapped 異常耐性 and 必殺耐性, and negative resistances. PC saves exported again match the original byte for byte, apart from the backlog text.
- **SystemAoi:** a save made in a dungeon no longer puts the party one cell behind.
- **SystemAoi:** after a lost battle, the battle closes by itself and the loss screens match the PC.

## Known limits

- Extra's ユニット図鑑, アイテム図鑑 and 迷宮踏破リスト aren't there yet.
- Text speeds other than 最速 and 普通 are inferred.
- Groups that appear partway through a visit, and what groups do after you flee, may differ from the PC.
- Enemies don't learn skills by level yet.
- 絶命院 is a little darker than on the PC.
- With touch, a tapped walking arrow stays drawn until the next touch.
- The limits listed for 0.60.0 to 0.60.10 still apply.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and compared with the original engine under Wine, with saves exchanged both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
