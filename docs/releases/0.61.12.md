False Myth 2 moves in half tiles, as on the PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.12-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.12.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.12-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **Wolf RPG Editor:** games set to move characters half a tile a step (False Myth 2) do so, with Wolf RPG Editor's rule that a character can't move while it overlaps a blocked quarter of a tile. Saves keep the half-tile positions both ways: a save made in VNDegen beside the Sanctuary's wall loads on the PC and walks, and the PC's saves load where they were made. Events placed half a tile up or left, touch ranges and the hero's whole-tile touch box work as on the PC, so False Myth 2's hero stops at the Sanctuary door and the dungeon sign where the PC's does.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** drawing a frame costs less: blending over the already opaque frame skips three divisions a pixel, frames clear a row at a time, and a zoomed map no longer clears the screen it then covers. False Myth 2's dungeon draws in about 12 ms a frame on the Linux test host where it took 15.

- **Wolf RPG Editor:** Confirm starts the first event the hero stands in or faces, so False Myth 2's dungeon sign reads.

## Known limits

- **False Myth 2:** past the South Tower after the first dungeon it still hasn't been played in VNDegen, and no shop has been reached.
- **False Myth 2:** its dungeons and battles still hitch for a moment every few seconds on the Mac (#206). The time goes into drawing a few large sprite sheets, most likely slowed by macOS memory compression on a busy Mac; that isn't proven, and a Mac or iPad with memory to spare hasn't been measured.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** from a half-tile position the PC refuses a whole-tile step, and VNDegen allows it.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
- A Mac or iPad without memory pressure.
