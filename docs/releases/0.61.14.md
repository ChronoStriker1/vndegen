False Myth 2 plays on past the South Tower.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.14-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.14.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.14-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **Wolf RPG Editor:** loading a save resets the hero's walk-through, fixed facing and height. False Myth 2 lets its hero walk through things in its dungeons and scenes, and after loading a save made elsewhere the hero walked straight past the dungeon sign and other events.

## Known limits

- **False Myth 2:** the story after the "Unwanted Deathmatch" battle at the South Tower hasn't been played in VNDegen, and no shop has been reached.
- **False Myth 2:** on a Mac busy with other work, dungeons and battles still hitch for a moment every few seconds. The app is mostly waiting then, not drawing (#206).
