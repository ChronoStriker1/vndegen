A new engine: ANIM, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Double Date Dilemma.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **ANIM**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is Double Date Dilemma: the title, the story with its choices, voices, music and movies, the flowchart, the backlog, quick save, and the Save and Load screens with Delete and Reset Saves.
- The E-mote characters are drawn through eluna, an MPL-2.0 Rust version of E-mote, with fixes of VNDegen's own listed in `Engines/ANIM/UPSTREAM.md`: expressions, blinking, hair physics, and the idle sway and gaze the PC version plays.
- Saves are the PC's own files, so a save made on a PC loads in VNDegen and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- [docs/anim.md](../anim.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- E-mote timelines and the scripts' short pose animations don't play; characters hold the scripted pose plus the idle sway.
- In the second swimsuit event, a few backdrop tiles are wrong.
- The Settings, Extra and shortcut screens, the AUTO SAVE page and comment editing on save cards aren't built yet, and volumes aren't kept between runs.
- Screen effects are approximations of the PC's.
- On the iPad simulator, three moving characters run at 35 to 45 frames a second; the Mac app holds 60.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
