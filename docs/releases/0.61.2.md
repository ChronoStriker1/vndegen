Sacred plays on Smile Game Builder, from its title through the first dungeon and the Vord forest out onto the world map.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.2-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **Sacred (セイクリッド～砕かれた光の冠～)** now plays on Mac, iPhone and iPad, from the title through its opening, the castle and the queen, the practice battle, the castle town and its weapon shop, and the first dungeon, 王家の洞窟, with the golems where アリア rejoins, the crown behind the stone bridge and the second cave, out through the Vord forest onto the world map. Saves in the royal cave were checked both ways with the PC. The game is in Japanese, as on the PC.

## Changed

- **Smile Game Builder:** map objects and 3D characters are lit as brightly as on the PC; they were about a quarter too dark.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the shop's quantity box is drawn in the middle of the screen as on the PC, and items you can't afford are greyed.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the item page shows weapons and armour plainly, with their description.

## Fixed

- **Smile Game Builder:** stone bridges can be walked on and under, as in Sacred's royal cave, where the bridge is the way to the crown.
- **Smile Game Builder:** an open door or a floor switch no longer blocks the way.
- **Smile Game Builder:** characters standing on stairs or ramps are saved at the height the PC saves, so soldiers no longer sink into the throne-room stairs after a load.
- **Smile Game Builder:** events that leave during a scene stay gone after a save and load, and a scene that moves only the player no longer puts everyone back where they started.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the escort soldier accepts the long sword when it's equipped.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the help bar no longer follows you from the castle into the town.
- **Smile Game Builder:** caves and dungeon cells without sunlight show black around the map, not the sky and the sea.

## Known limits

- **Sacred:** past the Vord forest (the world map, サンプール and its inn, the later dungeons and the ending) hasn't been played in VNDegen yet. Random encounters always bring one monster.
- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure:** the second half, from the walls on the road to the North Tower to the ending, hasn't been played in VNDegen yet. With four heroes in a battle, the fourth stands at the right edge of the screen.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads, and iPhone Duo.
- Sacred's first inn, which is in a later town, and random encounters with more than one monster.
- Whether the four-golem fight in the royal cave can be won at level 1 on the PC. It was won at level 4 in both, and VNDegen loses it at level 1.
