Fixes RPG Maker MZ games whose maps went black and sprites drew out of order on iPhone and iPad.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.15.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.15.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- RPG Maker MZ: maps and sprites stay correct after iPadOS or iOS reclaims graphics memory. Before, the map could go black and sprites drew in the wrong order, worse after each battle, until none showed, as in No Socks RPG. VNDegen now restores the tilemap and battle animations when the system gives the game its graphics back. A battle animation playing at that moment ends early.

## Known limits

- RPG Maker MV games aren't covered yet. One that loses its graphics the same way still goes black until it's restarted.
- No Socks RPG's cutscene movies with see-through backgrounds still show white on iPhone and iPad. A fix is in progress.
