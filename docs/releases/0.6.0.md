Adds KiriKiri games (KiriKiri 2 and KiriKiri Z) on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.6.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.6.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- KiriKiri games run inside VNDegen's window. VNDegen finds them by `data.xp3`, an unpacked `data` folder, or an archive appended to the game's `.exe`, up to two folders down.
- The VNDegen menu, the touch gamepad, touch mouse, controllers and the keyboard all work. With a controller in KAG games, A is Return, B is a right click, Y and Menu are Escape, X holds Control to skip, and LB and RB page through the message history.
- Encrypted archives work with no per-game setup. VNDegen recognizes the common ciphers and says when a game uses one it doesn't know.
- Common Windows plugins are built in, including transitions, layerExDraw, layerExSave, PSB and emote images, and the plugins in Steam builds.
- Movies play: MPEG-1 and MPEG-2, WMV, and H.264 and HEVC with hardware decoding.
- Older KiriKiri 2 games get the script behavior they were written for, and a game's `.cf` settings file applies, so a game that moves its save folder keeps it there.
- Saves stay where the PC version keeps them: `savedata`, a save folder set in the `.cf` file, or the AppData and Documents folders. Ludusavi backups from Windows and the Steam Deck map onto them.
- Quitting a game, or VNDegen itself, lets the game save its system data, even with one of its Yes/No dialogs open. You can play any number of KiriKiri games one after another without restarting VNDegen.

## Known limits

- Sound effects and music stored as MP3 don't play. MP3 audio inside movies does.
- AlphaMovie (`.amv`) animations don't show.
- In a game's Yes/No dialog, the first D-pad press highlights Yes, so press it twice to reach No before pressing A. A Windows keyboard behaves the same way.
- If a Mac logs out or shuts down while a KiriKiri game is still starting with a question on screen, the settings from that moment aren't kept.
- One game's save thumbnails were black in a test run. It didn't happen again.
- Tested with nine KiriKiri games from the library. Six reach their title, play, save and load on the iPhone simulator. On the Mac, four of them were checked through save and load, and the other two through reaching their title and quitting with their saves written. Not yet tested on a real device.
