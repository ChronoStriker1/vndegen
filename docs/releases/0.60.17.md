BUNNY BLACK 3's rest conversations, trap scenes, second-playthrough carry-over and scene backdrops, and Raidy saves that load in the PC original.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.17-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.17.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.17-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.





## Added

- **SystemAoi:** the rest conversations, which play when you rest before camp, and the trap scenes, which play after a trap's message.
- **SystemAoi:** carry-over to a second playthrough, kept in the same file the PC uses, so a carry-over registered on either side is offered on the other.
- **SystemAoi:** story intros show the area's backdrop picture, fading in as on the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** see-through opened crystals, weather particles, wall variants, and the status card's portrait as on the PC.

## Changed

- **SystemAoi:** random battles are fitted again from 72 PC trials, and 警戒心 and 引き寄せ change the rate as on the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** hidden doors open one way, from their own side.
- **SystemAoi:** the 開ける button stays until the item popup closes.

## Fixed

- **SystemAoi:** in a new game started in VNDegen, the boss-side scenes and trap scenes now play. A counter the PC updates on every step was never set.
- **Ikura:** saves from Lightning Warrior Raidy II and Raidy now load in the PC original. They name files with their extensions, as the PC does, and match its slots field for field (#200).

## Known limits

- **SystemAoi:** which wall variant the PC draws where wasn't found from play or data, so VNDegen picks one per wall that matches the PC for about a third of walls.
- **SystemAoi:** no encounter flash before a boss intro yet.
- **SystemAoi:** the limits listed for 0.60.0 to 0.60.15 still apply, except those fixed here.
- **Ikura:** a few bytes the PC fills with leftover memory differ in VNDegen's slots. They don't change where or how a slot loads.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. BUNNY BLACK 3 was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and compared with the original engine under Wine. The Ikura saves were checked in the PC original under Wine and in a headless build of the engine.
- No code review was done on this release.
