A fourth Tmr-Hiro game, Spooky Pregnant School: The Quickening, with its movies, and MP3 sound in MPEG-1 movies.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.49.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.49.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.49.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Spooky Pregnant School: The Quickening** plays, in English, through both routes to their endings: its title, the select and character maps, choices, its movies, picture effects, backlog, saving and loading. Its saves move both ways with a PC copy.
- Movies: MPEG-1 movies whose sound is MP3 now play with their sound, in every engine that uses the shared movie player. A click skips a Tmr-Hiro movie, as on the PC.
- Tmr-Hiro: the system screen's voice page sets each character's voice on or off and its volume, and keeps them with the game's own settings, where the PC keeps them.
- [docs/tmrhiro.md](../tmrhiro.md) has the details.

## Fixes

- Tmr-Hiro: in Kyonyuu Elf Oyako Saimin and Kyonyuu Hitozuma Onna Kyoushi Saimin, the system screen shows its buttons and sliders again (#184).
- Tmr-Hiro: Welcome to the Mizuryuu kei land's map bar no longer shows a quit button the PC doesn't have.

## Known limits

- The Quickening asks whether to read the first game's save; on the PC that opens a Windows folder picker. VNDegen goes on without it.
- The Quickening's black route calls a scene the game doesn't ship, and VNDegen passes it by. One voice line is stored under a name with a trailing space and doesn't play. What the PC does at these two places wasn't checked.
- The other Tmr-Hiro limits from earlier releases still apply.
- Spooky Pregnant School: The Conception, the last library Tmr-Hiro game, follows in a later release.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped. Portrait on the iPad wasn't confirmed.
- Both routes were played to their endings in the Linux test build, reaching 203 of the 279 story scenes; the rest are other characters' branches.
- Screens were compared with the PC under Wine, and saves were exchanged with it both ways.
- The opening movie played all its frames at its full 29.9 fps on the Mac app and the iPad simulator. Hanachirasu's opening movie decodes to the same pictures and sound as before.
- The last fixes (repeated labels, calls to missing scenes, picture layers in saves) were checked in the Linux test build and under Wine, not again in the apps.
- No code review was done on this release.
