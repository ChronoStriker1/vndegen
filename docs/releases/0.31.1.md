A Scene replay started from inside the story no longer loses the story, and Yabai! -Fukushuu Yami Site- gets its thought lines, its text speed and its Auto pace.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.31.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.31.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- NekoSDK, every game: a Scene replay started from a menu inside the story keeps the story. When the replay is over, Back from the Scene screen returns to the story where it was. Before, it landed on the title and progress since the last save was gone (#74). The story's Previous Choice is kept with it, and a save made during the replay holds the replay only.
- Yabai!: text comes out a character at a time, at the PC's pace at Config's default and at its slowest setting. Before, every line appeared at once.
- Yabai!: Auto waits on each line as the PC does at Config's default. Before, it moved on at once.
- Yabai!: the hub draws the pink thought lines that drift behind the bubbles, each heroine's own for each truth and stage.
- Yabai!: the hub's bubbles start in different places each time, as on the PC. They start apart and turn back as they touch, so a question is never hidden under another bubble.
- Yabai!: the rolls of the first and second endings pause 3.9 seconds before the fade, as timed on the PC.
- Yabai!: a save folder that VNDegen has written to keeps the PC's bulb flickering when it goes back to a PC.

## Known limits

- During a replay started inside the story, the story's music plays on until the replay's own script changes it.
- Yabai!'s text speed between the default and the fastest, and Auto away from its default, aren't timed on the PC yet.
- In Yabai!'s fifth truth the game's own list names six thought pictures the game doesn't have. Nothing shows in their turn; what the PC does there isn't checked.
- The vote's raised hands and a few of its timings are fitted, not measured.
- Mankitsu Happening's limits from 0.31.0 still stand.
- One NekoSDK game is still being built: Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used.
- The replay fix was run headless in Yabai! in four cases (from the hub, left by Exit, from inside a scene, and a save and load after coming back) and compared with the PC game under Wine. The other NekoSDK games are covered by the automated tests, not played.
- The Previous Choice part of the fix was added after those runs and is covered by an automated test.
- The Mac app and the iPad simulator were run on Yabai!'s hub and questions before the last review fixes, not after.
