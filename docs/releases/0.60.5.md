BUNNY BLACK 3's dungeon, ダークス宮, takes its turn: adventurers raid it, its floors grow to seven, and its posts gain experience.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.5-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.5.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.5-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.
## Added

- **SystemAoi:** from turn 42, ダークス宮 has its own screen in the town, with a tab bar and the 迷宮情報 and 迷宮職情報 windows. The cross-section shows each floor and the recent income, and 組織図 gains the dungeon posts.
- **SystemAoi:** 迷宮情報's numbers come from the post holders, the staffed facilities and the depth, and its marks and 評判 follow them as on the PC. A member whose ability matches their post shows the stat boost.
- **SystemAoi:** each turn, adventurers walk the floors and meet battles, traps, chests and 迷う events. Those who reach the bottom take a share of the town's food, money and resources, and the income box shows the result.
- **SystemAoi:** the dungeon deepens to seven floors as turns pass and its facilities and residents grow. Each floor widens with its 階層長's 統率, and 支援, 補佐 and 遊撃 open at four, five and six floors.
- **SystemAoi:** dungeon post holders gain experience from each intruder.
- Saves keep the dungeon's posts, its turn record and 最近の収支 both ways with the PC.

## Changed

- **SystemAoi:** the experience a unit needs to level up now follows the PC's table, in battles too.

## Fixed

- **SystemAoi:** the town's older buttons and windows sat 1 to 12 pixels off the PC's; they now match.

## Known limits

- The PC picks its random numbers in a way VNDegen doesn't copy, so the odds of each dungeon event, their outcomes and the chance of extra intruders were fitted to 330 turns of the PC. They match its mix of events but not its turn-by-turn results.
- The post holders' small figures on their floors aren't drawn.
- Posts don't change the town's own production yet, and facilities don't level up, so deeper floors come only from the number of facilities and residents.
- The limits listed for 0.60.0 to 0.60.4 still apply.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
