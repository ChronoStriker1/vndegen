A tap on the arrow beside a Config slider steps it by one in every NekoSDK game, and Funbag Fantasy 2's text types at the PC's pace.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.31.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.31.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- NekoSDK, every game: a tap or click on the minus or plus arrow beside a Config or Music slider or knob steps the setting by one. Before, a tap at the bar's height stepped it and then threw it to its lowest or highest value (#77). That was opacity, wait time, the volumes, and Text and Auto where they are bars, since the first NekoSDK release. A drag now moves only the slider it began on.
- Funbag Fantasy 2: text comes out a character at a time, at the pace timed on the PC, and Auto waits on each line. Before, every line appeared at once and Auto went straight on after a line with no voice (#76).
- Funbag Fantasy 2: Start on a fresh save folder goes straight into the prologue. Before, it could open an empty screen.
- Funbag Fantasy 2, Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story 2 and Mankitsu Happening: the wait-time knob takes a press only along its own travel.
- NekoSDK: a save made during a Scene replay no longer carries the last choice of the story it was started from, or of a replay before it.
- NekoSDK: a press on the Music screen's volume sets it without a drag (#78). Players never saw a fault here; the change goes with the slider fix.

## Known limits

- In Sideboob Story 2 and Mankitsu Happening, a press on the 60 pixels left of the wait-time knob's lowest place now does nothing. What the PC does with a click there wasn't checked.
- Which controls lie near a slider was measured in twelve games. Sideboob Story 2 and Mankitsu Happening weren't measured; their Config screens are laid out the same way.
- Yabai!'s and Mankitsu Happening's limits from 0.31.0 and 0.31.1 still stand.
- One NekoSDK game is still being built: Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used.
- The slider fix was tapped in Funbag Fantasy 2 only: in the Mac app with the mouse, and on the iPad simulator by touch, including a tap with the finger moving, a drag past the bar's end, and drags that drift up and down off the bar. The other games are covered by the automated tests, which now click the way the app does.
- A drag with the mouse in the Mac app wasn't done by hand; it was done on the simulator and in the headless build.
- Funbag Fantasy 2's text and Auto were looked at in the Mac app and timed in the headless build against the PC game under Wine.
- The last review fixes were tested but not read by a reviewer.
