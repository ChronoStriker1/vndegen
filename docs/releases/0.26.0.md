Ponscripter games now run, starting with the Umineko games. This release also brings a portrait layout for phones, with the game on top and the controls below, a layout pass for the iPhone Duo, and fixes for movie timing and cutscenes.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.26.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.26.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: Ponscripter games

- Ponscripter is NScripter with UTF-8 scripts and proportional English text. A game whose folder has `0.utf` runs in Ponscripter mode on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Both Umineko Arcs run, with their English and Japanese text, saves, movies and menus.
- Damaged or unusual font files no longer crash or stall the app. Fonts are checked before use, and drawing stays inside the file.

## Portrait and the iPhone Duo

- On a phone held upright, and on an iPad or iPad window taller than it is wide, the game sits at the top of the screen with the touch controls below it, so they no longer cover the game. On the iPhone Duo the game starts below the camera and the controls are centred.
- The home screen has one button for how the library looks. The All games screen is now the Grid layout, the fourth step of the view button.
- On the iPhone Duo's outer display, the library, its sheets, Settings and the VNDegen menu use the full width and keep only the camera's corner clear. Games held sideways keep clear of the camera.
- Folded, sheets open full height with Done in a full-width bar at the bottom. A confirmation stacks its buttons full width at the bottom, and a controller moves between them with Up and Down. Lists fade where they meet the bar.
- The game page names every button. Where one row can't, the folder, engine files and Remove buttons move to a labelled second row.
- The touch gamepad's keys are lighter, so more of the game shows through. The touch cursor marks its trackpad below the game.
- Settings has shorter footers and a narrower reading column on wide screens. The controller layout editor opens on the buttons, with the game's layout in one row.
- Saves shows a clear "Set up Ludusavi backups" prompt before any backup exists. Recently played is hidden while it would show only one game. Licenses reflow to the screen, and the passcode keypad fits short windows.

## Fixes

- No Socks RPG: cutscenes whose clips have transparency no longer show a white background on iPhone and iPad. (#44)
- Movies and timers in NScripter, Ponscripter, NekoSDK and Wolf RPG games run at the right speed on every display. Each screen refresh was rounded to whole milliseconds, so on a 120 Hz Mac or iPad movies played 4% slow, and on a 60 Hz display 2% fast. Video now keeps pace with its sound. (#49)
- Wolf RPG games run exactly one game frame per refresh at 60 Hz, or one every two at 120 Hz, with no skipped or doubled frames.
- NekoSDK: in The Alchemist & His Battle-Scarred Homunculus and Yarinaoshi no Christmas, Exit on the message bar during a replay from the Scene gallery goes back to the gallery, as on the PC, instead of closing the game. Loading a save ends a replay. (#45)
- Mac: the window always opens on screen. A window saved off screen or larger than the display is brought back and fitted to it. (#51)

## Known limits

- NekoSDK: scenes unlocked on a PC don't move with a backup yet. The Scene gallery shows those played in VNDegen. (#46, fixed in work that hasn't shipped yet)
- Ponscripter: glyph edges differ from the PC's by a few levels of grey, because VNDegen draws text with stb_truetype rather than FreeType. A font with reversed `loca` entries, or a `cmap` subtable longer than its table, is refused, where FreeType may accept it. None of 1,194 fonts tested does either.
- RPG Maker MV: with the pointer resting on No Socks RPG's Options title command, a click in cursor mode doesn't select it. A direct tap does. (#43)

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The No Socks RPG cutscene fix was checked on simulators. Please try the first cutscene on your iPhone Duo.
- The iPhone Duo layout was judged on the iPhone Duo simulator, with an ordinary iPhone and an iPad checked alongside. A real Duo's camera may sit a little differently.
- An unfolded Duo game in portrait couldn't be checked, because the simulator can't rotate it.
- The Mac app was checked on one Mac only.
