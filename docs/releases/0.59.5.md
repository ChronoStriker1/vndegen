SiglusEngine saves now go both ways with the PC version, and movie sound no longer cuts out early.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.5-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.5.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.5-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **SiglusEngine:** slots saved in VNDegen now load in the PC version of Harmonia and planetarian HD with their text, instead of crashing it or loading with no text. That holds for a new playthrough and for one continued from a PC save.
- **SiglusEngine:** planetarian HD's PC saves now load in VNDegen.
- **SiglusEngine:** loading a PC slot keeps the page's earlier lines in place, and the first line after loading is no longer indented on the PC. The Saves sheet no longer warns to back up PC saves.
- **Movies:** movies that store their sound behind the picture play all of it. planetarian HD's opening had lost its last 4 seconds of sound.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and against the PC version under Wine.
- The movie fix was measured, not heard: the test Mac had no sound capture. NekoSDK's movies weren't re-tested.
- No code review was done on this release.
