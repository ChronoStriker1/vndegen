ExHibit's Extra menu for Succubus Sessions: Mami Mamiya's Sweet Slice of Hell, with the CG gallery, scene replay and music room.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.55.3-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.55.3.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.55.3-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- The title's Extra now opens, with the CG, Scene and Music pages drawn from the game's own pictures and laid out as on the PC.
- CG: a group opens once one of its pictures has been shown, and the viewer steps through all of the group's pictures. On a touch screen, a tap on the bottom strip brings back a hidden viewer bar.
- Scene: a scene opens once it has been played through in the story. A replay plays the scene and returns to the Scene page.
- Music: all 21 tracks, with a volume bar.

## Known limits

- Extra counts only what was seen in VNDegen. What a PC copy has seen isn't read.
- The CG viewer has no Full Screen or Original buttons.
- Return to title from the system menu doesn't ask first, as the PC does.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Extra was checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator by touch.
- No code review was done on this release.
