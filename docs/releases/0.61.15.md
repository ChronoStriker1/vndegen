## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.15-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.15.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.15-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **Smile Game Builder:** battles take the light of the map the party is on, as on the PC: Sacred's campfire battle with the dragon girl shows bright snow, and battles in dark places such as Sacred's 王家の洞窟 and Colette's ドラコ room are as dark as on the PC.
- **Sacred:** ロイ appears in battle with his knight model (#207).
- **Smile Game Builder:** skill damage and healing follow the PC's rule, the skill's power plus a share of the target's maximum HP, without defence or magic: Sacred's ドラゴンサンダー does 124 as on the PC (#205), and Colette's heroes' skills and heals match the PC's numbers.

## Known limits

- **Sacred:** 雪山 is darker than on the PC, the walls behind 王家の洞窟's battles are brown where the PC's are grey stone, and the dragon girl's model is drawn flat and pale where the PC shades it.
- **Smile Game Builder:** how often a status ailment lands still isn't measured on the PC (#205).
