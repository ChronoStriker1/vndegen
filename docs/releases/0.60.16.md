Two fixes from the regression sweep: Raidy II's opening saves load with the desert, and tapping back from RPG Maker MZ's Options no longer starts a new game.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.16-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.16.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.16-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.





## Fixed

- **Ikura:** in Lightning Warrior Raidy II, a save made in the opening after the logo now loads with the desert behind it, not the logo on black (#198). Saves made before this update, and the PC's own saves, still load with the logo on black, as they do on the PC.
- **RPG Maker MV and MZ:** on iPhone and iPad, a tap reaches the game a frame after the pointer moves to it, as a mouse click does. In Fallen Kunoichi, tapping the return arrow in Options no longer also starts New Game (#199).

## Known limits

- **Ikura:** a VNDegen save from Raidy II's opening doesn't load in the PC original yet (#200).
- **RPG Maker MZ:** after choosing a command on Fallen Kunoichi's title by touch, tap the title away from the commands before choosing another. The game expects a mouse to cross that area, and does the same on the PC.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The Ikura fix was checked in the Mac app and against the PC original under Wine. The touch fix was checked on iPhone and iPad simulators, with Fallen Kunoichi (MZ) and Rookie Knight Rathi (MV).
- No code review was done on this release.
