Fixes Touch mouse mode on iPhone and iPad, and a black screen after quitting a game on a landscape iPhone. Ren'Py games unpacked two folders deep are now found.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.4.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.4.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixed

- Touch mouse mode now works in games that turn off the mouse when it stops moving, such as RPG Maker games with a modern mouse plugin. VNDegen now moves the game's mouse to each touch as it starts, so taps land where you touch.
- On a landscape iPhone, tapping Quit to library in the VNDegen menu no longer leaves every later game on a black screen until the app restarts.
- In a short, narrow window, such as a small Mac window, the VNDegen menu keeps one column instead of cutting off its controls at the sides.
- Ren'Py games unpacked two folders deep, such as `Game/Game-1.0-pc/renpy`, are now found when the library is scanned.

## Changed

- On iPhone and iPad, a Ren'Py game's page no longer offers a control method. Ren'Py takes touches directly there, so the choice did nothing. Button mapping is still there.
- VNDegen's own code is now under the MIT License.

## Known limits

- In games that turn off the mouse when it stops moving, a touch held still for more than two seconds stops working until the next tap, as a still mouse does on a PC.
- The Touch mode fix was tested on the iPhone and iPad simulators, not yet on a real device.
- Save sync still connects over SSH in this release. Connecting to an SMB share is in progress.
