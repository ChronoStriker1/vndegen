The fifth Tmr-Hiro game, Spooky Pregnant School: The Conception, and The Quickening's routes follow the player's choices.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.50.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.50.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.50.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Spooky Pregnant School: The Conception** plays, in English, from the prologue through its chapter maps to its ending: the clock and stamina gauge, items and their tutorial, the status screen, the roulette, saving and loading. Its saves move both ways with a PC copy. All five library Tmr-Hiro games now play.
- In The Conception's item tutorial, clicking the bottle or the status button over the text uses it, as the script means. In the PC engine, run under Wine, those buttons never answer during the text and the tutorial never ends.
- [docs/tmrhiro.md](../tmrhiro.md) has the details.

## Fixes

- Tmr-Hiro: in Spooky Pregnant School: The Quickening, the choices now decide the route. Before, every new game took the white route (#186).

## Known limits

- At The Conception's ending, the PC offers to write a save that The Quickening can import. VNDegen skips that step; returning to the title works.
- The Conception's chapter 3 can lock: map 13 shows no characters if the character skipped on map 11 is the one it waits for. The PC locks the same way from the same point. Loading an earlier save gets out.
- After loading a save in The Conception, the clock shows again only when the story next draws it.
- A few sounds the game's archives don't hold play nothing: one is a typo in the script, and some voices were never shipped.
- The other Tmr-Hiro limits from earlier releases still apply.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- One play-through reached the ending in the Linux test build: 219 story scenes. Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- The Quickening's four ways into its two routes were each played to their ending in the Linux test build. The other three Tmr-Hiro games were played again to their staff rolls.
- No code review was done on this release.
