BUNNY BLACK 3 recruits: new units join as the town grows, and beaten area bosses bring their leaders over.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.2-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **SystemAoi:** BUNNY BLACK 3's new units join as the town's housing grows, shown in the PC's 部下が増えました。 box with their cards. サキュー, whose 感知 opens 炎山洞's hidden doors, joins once the town has a level 3 豪邸住居.
- **SystemAoi:** area bosses stand guard in their areas, and beating one brings its leader over.
- **SystemAoi:** building something for the first time plays the carpenters' scene.

## Fixed

- **SystemAoi:** the town's turn events, and mission deadlines, come on the same turn as on the PC. They had come a turn early.

## Known limits

- Where the strongholds' bosses stand comes from the game's tables and maps; it hasn't been checked against the original yet.
- A failed test mission ends the game, as the game's scripts say; that wasn't played on the PC.
- The town's defence and 解任/編成 come later. The limits listed for 0.60.0 and 0.60.1 still apply.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The game was played in the Linux test build and the Mac app, and saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways. The iPad simulator wasn't rerun, since nothing in input or drawing changed.
- No code review was done on this release.
