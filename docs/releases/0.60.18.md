BUNNY BLACK 3's enemy symbols in the dungeons, the PC's battle rewards and town growth, exact sprites and portraits, and RPG Maker key repeat that matches the PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.18-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.18.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.18-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.





## Added

- **SystemAoi:** enemy groups show their symbols in the 3D view, as on the PC, and an encounter holds briefly, then crossfades into the battle.
- **SystemAoi:** saves keep opened doors, and pickups drop on the way home, as on the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** learned skills carry over to a second playthrough.
- **SystemAoi:** the three 3D settings in Option work.

## Changed

- **SystemAoi:** battle rewards follow the PC's per-enemy rule, fitted from 62 PC wins, with food and money. A second playthrough's reward setting scales them.
- **SystemAoi:** town posts and the 街長 add the PC's rolls each turn, and the town values the PC works out on load are kept.
- **SystemAoi:** sprite sheets are cut by their own grid in the diorama, 配置 and battles, and portraits sit on their anchors, so every figure and face lands on the PC's pixels.
- **SystemAoi:** battle pacing follows ゲーム全体の速度, notices close after a time set by their length, and the choice box, message window and font match the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** a save repeats the same random rolls when loaded.
- **RPG Maker XP, VX and VX Ace:** a held direction or button repeats on the same frames as on the PC, one frame sooner than before (#201).

## Fixed

- **SystemAoi:** after walking through a door, the view shows the corridor instead of the door filling the screen.
- **SystemAoi:** the 建設 card and board, the town LOG, and the town help backdrops match the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** snowflakes loop.

## Known limits

- **SystemAoi:** the PC's random rolls depend on something outside the save, so VNDegen's rolls differ from the PC's even from the same save.
- **SystemAoi:** a second target of 乱舞斬 or 火矢三連 takes half damage, where the PC was seen dealing full damage. ホリィ's pre-round 自動攻撃 and the 戦闘隊列 そのまま option aren't there yet.
- **SystemAoi:** the 図鑑 and the 迷宮's post holders still cut their sprite sheets the old way, and in 配置 a held figure is drawn in front where the PC draws it behind.
- **SystemAoi:** Option's autosave mode and popup settings do nothing yet, and its tooltips aren't drawn.
- **SystemAoi:** VNDegen opens a key door after a dungeon save is loaded; the PC refuses.
- **SystemAoi:** the limits listed for 0.60.0 to 0.60.17 still apply, except those fixed here.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. BUNNY BLACK 3 was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and compared with the original engine under Wine. The RPG Maker key repeat was measured on the PC engines under Wine and checked in the Mac app with INMA KA SUGOROKU and Lust Doll.
- No code review was done on this release.
