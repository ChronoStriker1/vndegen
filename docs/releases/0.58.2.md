Wolf RPG pictures now come back after loading a save, as on the PC, and picture angles match the PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.58.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.58.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.58.2-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **Wolf RPG:** President Yukino, Drain Dungeon and Journey of Depravity bring back the pictures that were on screen when you load a save, as their PC versions do. In President Yukino that is the day bar and portrait. Their saves keep the pictures both ways with the PC.
- **Wolf RPG:** picture angles are in tenths of a degree, as on the PC, so turned and spinning pictures, such as Parade Buster's target marker, turn by the right amount.

## Known limits

- Picture effects (shake, pulse, automatic patterns), XY and cut images, and text pictures with control codes other than a leading font size aren't restored from saves.
- Seris saves pictures in a newer layout, which VNDegen doesn't read yet.
- Games on older Wolf engines (ANGEL QUEST, Milky Quest, Parade Buster, Succubus Forest) get no pictures in their saves, since their PC versions don't restore them, or close when a save holds one.
- President Yukino's camera after a load still differs from the PC, as before.

## What wasn't tested

- The iPad simulator. The changes were checked in the Mac app, the Linux test build and against the PC versions under Wine.
- Milky Quest's PC version wouldn't start under Wine, so what it saves wasn't checked.
- No code review was done on this release.
