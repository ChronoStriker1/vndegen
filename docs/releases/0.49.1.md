Fixes for Tmr-Hiro games: The Quickening's side menu, and line wrapping.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.49.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.49.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.49.1-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixes

- Tmr-Hiro: in Spooky Pregnant School: The Quickening, the menu at the right edge of the screen (Save, Load, System, Title, End) is back, so the save and load pages open during the story again. Moving the pointer to the edge opens it; on a touchscreen, the first tap at the edge opens it and the next one picks (#185).
- Tmr-Hiro: long lines wrap where the PC wraps them, and commas and closing brackets no longer start a new line. This mainly changes Kyonyuu Hitozuma Onna Kyoushi Saimin and The Quickening.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The side menu was checked in the Mac app and, by touch, on an iPad simulator. The wrapping was checked in the Mac app in Kyonyuu Hitozuma Onna Kyoushi Saimin and against the PC under Wine, not in Kyonyuu Elf Oyako Saimin or Welcome to the Mizuryuu kei land.
- All four Tmr-Hiro games were played again to their endings in the Linux test build.
- No code review was done on this release.
