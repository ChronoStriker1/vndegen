Gives sound to RPG Maker MV games that ship only Ogg audio.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.8.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.8.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixed

- RPG Maker MV games that ship only Ogg audio, which is most of them, now have sound on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Before, VNDegen asked them for `.m4a` files they don't have, so they played silent. Encrypted audio and looping music work. MV games that ship `.m4a` files and RPG Maker MZ games play as before.

## Known limits

- Tested with an MV game with encrypted audio and one with plain Ogg files on the Mac and the iPad simulator. Music, background sounds, jingles and sound effects all play, and both hold about 60 fps on the map and in battle.
