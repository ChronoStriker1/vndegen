Save fixes for The Alchemist & His Battle-Scarred Homunculus and Yarinaoshi no Christmas.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.26.3-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.26.3.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- A save made on a PC with a comment typed on its card showed as an empty slot and couldn't be loaded. It loads now, and its comment shows on the card. (#61)

## New

- Save cards can be locked, as on the PC. A locked save can be loaded but not overwritten or deleted, and Quick Save skips it. Locks set on a PC carry over in a backup, and the other way round.
- The NEW mark shows on the save made last, kept with the game's settings as on the PC.
- The confirmation questions have the PC's "Don't ask again" box. Ticking it turns that confirmation off, whichever answer you give, and Config turns it back on.
- A card's lock and Delete button can be reached with a keyboard or controller.

## Known limits

- Comments can't be typed in VNDegen yet.
- Scenes unlocked on a PC don't move with a backup yet. The Scene gallery shows those played in VNDegen. (#46)

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Locks, the last-saved mark and comments were checked against the PC games under Wine, and by tests.
