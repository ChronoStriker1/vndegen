Adds AliceSoft System 3 games on Mac, iPhone and iPad, on VNDegen's own runtime.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.13.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.13.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: AliceSoft System 3

- AliceSoft's System 3 games run on a runtime written in C for VNDegen from AliceSoft's published System 3.5 and 3.9 SDKs, the games' files and the original engines' behaviour, MIT licensed like the rest of the app. `docs/system3-cleanroom.md` lists its sources.
- It runs the System 3.5 to 3.9 games (`System39.exe`), such as Rance, Kichikuou Rance, Rance III and IV, Toushin Toshi I and II, Mamatoto and Kaeru nyo Panyo~n. It also runs the Windows 3.1 `SYSTEM3.EXE` games, such as Rance 4.1 and 4.2, with the English text their `replacement.txt` gives.
- VNDegen finds a game by `System39.ain` beside its scenario archive, or by `ADISK.DAT` beside `SYSTEM3.EXE`, up to two folders down.
- Saves are the PC engines' own files in the game's folder, byte for byte what `System39.exe` and `SYSTEM3.EXE` write, so they move between VNDegen and a PC copy of the game. Toushin Toshi II loads saves made on the PC.
- CD music plays from the MP3 tracks the game's `_inmm.ini` lists, MIDI music plays through the SoundFont in Settings > MIDI music, and the System 3.0 games' own characters (`GAIJI.DAT`) show.
- Files are found whatever case the game names them in.

## Known limits: AliceSoft System 3

- Mamatoto's scripted tutorial stops before the first save point, so its saves haven't been checked.
- Text appears all at once rather than at the game's message speed, and some transitions show their end state without animating.
- System 3.0's own music format (`AMUS.DAT`) isn't played. The test games use CD tracks instead.
- The number input box takes its default, so Panyon's shop buys its default quantity.
- Ludusavi's manifest doesn't list these games, so Ludusavi doesn't find their saves on its own.
- Tested with 10 test games. All reach their title and play on the Mac and the iPad simulator, 9 save and load there, one also runs on the iPhone simulator, and Panyon's battles hold 60 fps. `docs/system3.md` has the details.
