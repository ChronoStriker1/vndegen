A new engine: VisualArt's SiglusEngine, on siglus_rs, with planetarian HD and Harmonia.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.43.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.43.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.43.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **SiglusEngine**, on siglus_rs, an open-source Rust reimplementation of the engine (MPL-2.0), on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Tested with the English Steam releases of planetarian HD and Harmonia: title, story with text, voices, music and movies, the games' own menus, Config, saving and loading.
- A game that ships several languages runs in the first of your preferred languages (iOS and macOS Settings) that it has, and in English when none matches.
- English text wraps by whole words, where the original engine breaks lines by character.
- Saves are the PC engine's own files in the PC's folder beside the game, such as `savedata_en/`. Saves made on a PC load in VNDegen.
- Movies: `.omv` movies play through siglus_rs's own decoder, and `.wmv` movies, such as planetarian HD's opening, through the FFmpeg the app already ships for KiriKiri.
- SiglusEngine games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/siglus.md](../siglus.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- A save made in VNDegen can crash the PC version when loaded there, or load there without its text. The game's Saves sheet warns about it. Saves made on a PC load in VNDegen, and the settings and read-text files move both ways.
- There is no per-game language choice yet; the language follows your system's preferred languages.
- Sound keeps playing, muted, while VNDegen's menu is open.
- The games' own gamepad prompts don't show, since VNDegen sends them keys and clicks.
- Network features, such as planetarian HD's Tweet button, do nothing.
- On an iPhone, the touch gamepad covers part of Harmonia's save slots.
- RealLive games, which siglus_rs also runs, aren't included yet.
- The iPhone and iPad download is 5.6 MB larger.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Both games were played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, also with every file name's case swapped. Harmonia was also played on an iPhone simulator.
- Saves were exchanged with Harmonia's own engine under Wine. planetarian HD's engine starts in Chinese under Wine, so its saves weren't checked there.
- planetarian HD's opening movie was measured at 60 fps on the Mac app and the iPad simulator.
- Neither game was played to an ending.
- No code review was done on this release.
