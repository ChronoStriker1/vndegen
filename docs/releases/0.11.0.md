Adds Wolf RPG Editor games on Mac, iPhone and iPad, lets you choose the SoundFont for MIDI music, and shows screenshots on game pages.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.11.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.11.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: Wolf RPG Editor

- Wolf RPG Editor 2 and 3 games run on VNDegen's own Wolf RPG Editor runtime, written in C++ for VNDegen and MIT licensed. No open-source runtime existed, so this one was written from the games' files, permissively licensed format notes and the original engine's behaviour. `docs/wolfrpg-cleanroom.md` lists its sources.
- VNDegen finds a game by `Data.wolf`, `Data/BasicData` or `Data/BasicData.wolf` beside `Game.exe`, up to two folders down. It reads loose files, Wolf RPG Editor 2's `.wolf` archives and its encrypted basic data.
- The runtime runs the games' event scripts, including the standard systems' menus, shops and battles. It draws each frame on the CPU and shows it through Metal, and plays Ogg, MP3, WAV and MIDI music.
- Saves are the PC engine's own `Save/SaveDataNN.sav` files beside `Game.exe`. Saves made on the PC load in VNDegen, and the other way round, so Ludusavi backups work for these games.
- Wolf RPG Editor games start with the gamepad layout: A confirms, B and Menu cancel and open the menu, and Dash is the sub key. The touch gamepad and the keyboard work too.
- Files are found whatever case the game names them in.

## New: MIDI music

- Wolf RPG Editor games play their MIDI music through a General MIDI SoundFont that VNDegen includes, FluidR3 Mono (MIT).
- Settings > MIDI music lets you choose your own `.sf2` or `.sf3` SoundFont instead. VNDegen keeps a copy of it, and Use the built-in SoundFont goes back. If your SoundFont can't be read, the built-in one plays. RPG Maker XP, VX and VX Ace games play MIDI with a synthesizer of their own and aren't affected.

## Game pages

- A game's About section shows a row of screenshots from Steam, or from IGDB when Steam has none. Its description and genres also come from Steam first, then IGDB. Games you matched before this update pick these up the first time you open their page.
- The artwork picker no longer shows the same picture twice when IGDB, VNDB or SteamGridDB hosts a copy of Steam's image. IGDB's thumbnails in the picker are sharper, and its hero images are no longer cropped.
- On iPhone in portrait, the art at the top of the home screen and of game pages is shorter, so the shelves and the game's title come into view sooner.

## Known limits: Wolf RPG Editor

- Wolf RPG Editor 3.5's compressed data, DxLib archive versions 7 and 8, and Wolf RPG Editor Pro's encrypted data aren't read yet.
- Mouse input and CSV import aren't supported, so games that need the mouse can't be played.
- Typing text on the keyboard isn't supported. Games that offer a default, such as a name, go on with it.
- A load restores the map, characters and variables, but not pictures, the screen tint or the music that was playing. The map's own music starts, as on the PC.
- Tested with 3 test games, which reach their title, play, save and load on the Mac and the iPad simulator, pass the case check, and hold 60 fps in battle. One also plays on the iPhone simulator. 13 more library games load and play in the Linux test build. `docs/wolfrpg.md` has the details.
