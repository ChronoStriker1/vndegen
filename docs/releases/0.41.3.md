Web games no longer offer their movies and sound to AirPlay on their own.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.41.3-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.41.3.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- The web view that runs RPG Maker MV and MZ, TyranoScript, Visual Novel Maker, Twine and Flash games no longer allows AirPlay for a page's movies and sound. It was allowed by default, so iOS could send a game's movie to a TV apart from the rest of the picture. Playing on a TV is done with Screen Mirroring, which sends picture and sound together.
- Nothing else in VNDegen or its engines asks for AirPlay.

## Known limits

- If an AirPlay speaker or TV is picked as the device's sound output in Control Center, iOS sends VNDegen's sound there, as it does for every app. An app can't refuse that.
- Violated Hero was seen to stop partway through its opening (#85). It hasn't been reproduced since and is still open.

## What wasn't tested

- No AirPlay device was used. The check is the setting itself, the package tests, and the Mac and iOS builds.
- No game was played for this release.
- No physical device or hardware controller was used.
- No code review was done on this release.
