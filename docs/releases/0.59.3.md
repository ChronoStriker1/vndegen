ANIM's characters animate beside the story, and its Extra screen gains the Animation page and the Sprites viewer.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.3-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.3.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.3-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **ANIM:** the characters' short scripted animations, such as nods and moves, now play alongside the story, as on the PC. Before, the story paused for them and nothing moved while text showed.
- **ANIM:** the Extra screen's Animation page in both games. A scene loop is unlocked as you reach it in the story, and what is unlocked is shared with the PC version both ways.
- **ANIM:** the Extra screen's Sprites viewer in both games: pick a character, expression, outfit, background and arm pose, set the face and body sliders, switch the effects on and off, and save and load poses. Saved poses are shared with the PC version both ways, and the camera button writes a picture as the PC does.

## Known limits

- Two rarely used animation commands are skipped.
- In the Sprites viewer, the goddess's eyes in Magical Sword show sparkles over half-closed lids where the PC shows open eyes. The folder button does nothing, and Idle Motion isn't saved with a pose.
- The PC's own movie player crashed under Wine, so what a click does on the Animation page was matched to the rest of the game rather than checked.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch, and against the PC version under Wine.
- No code review was done on this release.
