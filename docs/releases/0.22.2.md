Monster Girl Quest Paradox now runs, looping video clips no longer freeze on an iPad simulator, files named with Windows backslashes load, and hub buttons lose the short vertical lines at their ends. These come from the frame rate check of every engine plugin, and [frame-rate.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/frame-rate.md) has its results.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.22.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.22.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- RPG Maker VX Ace: Monster Girl Quest Paradox runs. Script sections that start with a byte order mark load, script errors name their section and message, data objects made by scripts keep their values, and games that rename temporary saves can save.
- RPG Maker MV and MZ: looping WebM clips pause for about 34 to 125 ms at each turn instead of about 500 ms, and clips with Vorbis sound no longer freeze on an iPad simulator.
- TyranoScript: looping video clips no longer freeze on an iPad simulator, and pause for about 300 ms at each loop on a Mac instead of 500 to 600 ms.
- RPG Maker MV and MZ, TyranoScript, and Flash: files a game names with Windows backslashes load. This brings back the videos in "I fell in love with the the Demon Queen... But now I have to share her".
- KiriKiri: Taimanin Kurenai no longer reports a false "unknown encryption" problem for its movie archive. Its movies were never encrypted.
- Hub: buttons and tags no longer show short vertical lines at their ends.

## Known limits

- Monster Girl Quest Paradox's save list thumbnails are black. Saves and loads work.
- In a WebM with Vorbis sound, a seek to anywhere but the start restarts the clip on an iPad simulator.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Whether a real iPad freezes on Vorbis clips at all is unknown. The lines on hub buttons were reported on a real iPhone and iPad, and could only be reproduced in the Mac app's debug `-snapshot` picture, so the fix is checked there and not on a device.
- Monster Girl Quest Paradox: every battle was started by script, and a menu save in the Mac app wasn't tried. Later game content wasn't reached.
- The Demon Queen game: one event's clip was played, and the other clips' files were checked to exist. Flash wasn't run with the backslash fix.
- Taimanin Kurenai's movies were played from a script calling the game's own movie command, not through the story.
- The last change to the MV and MZ clip reload was checked in the app and has no separate code review.
