Fixes for button mapping and touch controls.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.2.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.2.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.

## Fixed

- Keys no longer stay stuck when you open the VNDegen menu while holding a button. The game gets the release, and a button still held when you resume does nothing until you press it again.
- Two buttons mapped to the same key keep it held until both are released, on the overlay and on a controller, including a button mapped to Enter alongside Confirm.
- A controller button mapped to Click clicks where the mouse pointer is.
- Your choice from the old "Touch controls" setting carries over to the new control method.

## Known limits

- If the VNDegen menu opens while a finger is held on the touch mouse, the game can see the mouse button as still pressed until the next click.
- The right stick cannot be mapped yet.
