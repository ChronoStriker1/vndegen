BUNNY BLACK 3's dungeon floors, ceilings and pillars use the original's texture variants.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.7-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.7.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.7-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **BUNNY BLACK 3:** dungeon floors, ceilings and pillars now use the same texture variants as the original, and pillars stand at the same corners.

## Known limits

- **BUNNY BLACK 3:** when a pillar's texture changes as the party steps isn't known from the PC; VNDegen changes it as the step starts. A few distant parts the PC leaves out are still drawn.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
