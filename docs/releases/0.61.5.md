Princess Colette's Great Adventure's optional dungeons play through, and particle effects such as the walls of fire and lightning are drawn.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.5-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.5.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.5-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure:** the optional places play through: ヤーマ岬 to its minotaur, the golem cave past its walls of fire and lightning to its boss and the moon flowers, and the クサレィ catacombs with the key their old keeper sells. The post-game tower's first lap plays to its 15th-floor boss. Saves at the golem cave and the catacombs were checked both ways with the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** particle effects are drawn: the walls of fire and lightning on the world map, the golem cave's flame and the sewer's dark fire.

## Changed

- **Smile Game Builder:** battles' skill and item lists are the PC's two-column grid, with a cost and description box and a やめる button.
- **Smile Game Builder:** in battles with three or four heroes, the first two stand in front, as on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** models take their own colours, so the cat's mansion's book spines and buildings' shading match the PC. The poison balloon's bubbles come and go as on the PC.

## Fixed

- **Smile Game Builder:** the player can no longer walk onto the sea, lava or ice where the PC stops them.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the golem boss in Colette's golem cave no longer comes back after its scene to block the moon flowers.
- **Smile Game Builder:** the cliffs and water at the edges of noon-sky maps are drawn, and the round shadow under map objects such as the tower's warp gates is gone.

## Known limits

- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure:** the post-game tower's laps after the first, ドラコ and the prison scene haven't been played in VNDegen yet.
- **Sacred:** from the volcano's fight with six 炎の悪魔 on hasn't been played in VNDegen yet. Its battle lists now use the two-column grid, which hasn't been compared with Sacred on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder saves from 0.61.0 to 0.61.3** take a worn item's HP or MP bonus off once when loaded, as in 0.61.4.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
- iPad simulator frame times for the tower floors past 6F.
