A new engine: ExHibit, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Succubus Sessions: Mami Mamiya's Sweet Slice of Hell.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.54.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.54.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.54.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **ExHibit**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is Succubus Sessions: Mami Mamiya's Sweet Slice of Hell, in English at 1920x1080: its title with music, the whole story to the ending with voices, sprites, camera moves, transitions and speech bubbles.
- The game's own message window and control bar, system menu, save and load pages with thumbnails, backlog and five settings pages are drawn from its own layouts, so they look as on the PC.
- ExHibit saves are the PC engine's own files in the game's `userdata` folder. A save made on a PC loads in VNDegen on the same line, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- ExHibit games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/exhibit.md](../exhibit.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- The small emotion icons on sprites, such as sweat drops and "!" marks, aren't drawn.
- The title's Extra menu (CG gallery, music room, scene replay) is greyed out.
- The backlog's Jump buttons aren't shown, and the window size and full screen settings do nothing; the app sets the window.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- The whole story was skipped through to the ending in the Linux test build. The Mac app and the iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and loading it after a restart.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- Sound wasn't listened to on the Mac or iPad.
- No code review was done on this release.
