A new engine: Ikura GDL, on VNDegen's own runtime, with the English Lightning Warrior Raidy. Games on CD images in BIN/CUE form can now be imported.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.52.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.52.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.52.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Ikura GDL**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is the English release of Lightning Warrior Raidy: its title, intro and story with voices, music and sound, choices, the 3D dungeon with its map, compass and events, the item, equipment and status menus, and battles.
- The game's own message window and its buttons, Skip and Auto, save and load pages and options page are drawn from its own parts, so they look as on the PC.
- Ikura GDL saves are the PC engine's own file in the game folder. A save made on a PC loads in VNDegen on the same screen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- **BIN/CUE disc images** can be imported: choose the `.cue` (on an iPhone or iPad outside VNDegen's own folder, choose the `.bin` too). A CD that starts an installer and keeps the game in one folder, as Raidy's does, becomes a game of just that folder. [docs/library.md](../library.md) has the details.
- [docs/ikura.md](../ikura.md) has the details of the engine.

## Known limits

- Ikura GDL has no backlog yet: the window's Log button and the mouse wheel do nothing.
- Read marks aren't kept, so Skip skips all text.
- The effect speed setting doesn't shorten fades.
- The 3D view's lighting and texture filtering are close to the PC's, not identical.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator.
- In the Mac app and the iPad simulator it was checked from the title through the story into the dungeon, with saves in the story and in the dungeon, quitting, and loading them again. A dungeon save made on the iPad loaded on the Mac.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- Sound wasn't listened to on the Mac or iPad.
- No code review was done on this release.
