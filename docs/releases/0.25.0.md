Unity games now open on a Mac: in their own Mac version when they ship one, or the Windows version through your CrossOver, in a bottle VNDegen keeps apart from yours.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.25.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.25.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: Unity games on a Mac

- A Unity game's Play button says "Open Mac version" when the game ships one, such as SiNiSistar 2, or "Play in CrossOver" otherwise. The game runs in its own window, outside VNDegen's menu, controls, and cheats. VNDegen notices when it quits, records play time, and backs up its saves.
- CrossOver is yours to install: VNDegen doesn't include it. The first Play makes one bottle of VNDegen's own, copies your Mac's standard fonts into it so menus have their text, and never opens, changes, or runs anything in your other bottles or CrossOver's settings. Rosetta 2 is needed too.
- Saves in the game's `AppData/LocalLow` folder, and the game's PlayerPrefs, which Windows keeps in the registry, back up and restore with Ludusavi. Only the game's own registry key is ever read or written, and every other key in a backup stays as it was.
- A game run through CrossOver can reach the network, as it would on a PC.
- iPhone and iPad keep the explanation of why Unity games can't run there.

## Known limits

- Some games draw part of a scene only in the window's top-left corner when their window is smaller than the screen. Full screen, which they start in, is fine.
- If a backup's registry holds two value names that differ only in how an accent is written, a backup that updates the game's PlayerPrefs keeps only one of them, in issue 40. No library game is known to do this.
- Video in some games falls back to slower software decoding under CrossOver.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The launcher was checked on one MacBook with CrossOver Preview, on three Windows games and SiNiSistar 2's Mac version, not across the library.
- PlayerPrefs restore, backup, undo, and older YAML registry backups were checked by tests, not with a real Ludusavi share.
- The last of three review rounds' fixes were covered by tests and one in-app check, and weren't reviewed again.
