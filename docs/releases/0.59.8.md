EAGLS, ExHibit and Bruns gain the screens and dialogs they were missing, and EAGLS and ExHibit share what you've seen with the PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.8-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.8.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.8-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **EAGLS:** the CGs and scenes you've seen are shared with the PC's `GlobalSave.sav`, both ways.
- **EAGLS:** name entry works without a hardware keyboard.
- **EAGLS:** the title's Settings and Exit buttons work. Before, Settings opened the exit box and Exit did nothing.
- **EAGLS:** Settings gains the Screen and System tabs: screen effects, left click to skip effects, and Reset Config.
- **EAGLS:** the mosaic, dissolve, wipe and blinds transitions are drawn as on the PC.
- **EAGLS:** Oppai no Ouja 48's Oppai Gallery, Oppai View, Oppai Scouter and champion bra page work.
- **ExHibit:** Extra shares the CGs and scenes you've seen with the PC, both ways.
- **ExHibit:** the game asks before returning to the title, ending, quick loading or overwriting a save.
- **ExHibit:** the CG viewer has the PC's controls, and the backlog has Jump.
- **Bruns:** the licence dialog shows on first start, and saves take a comment, as on the PC.
- **Bruns:** the backlog's spacing matches the PC, its voice replay buttons show, and a load keeps the loaded page in the backlog.

## Known limits

- **EAGLS:** what you've seen is written to `GlobalSave.sav` only once the PC version has started once with that folder.
- **ExHibit:** MANPU icons aren't drawn.
- **Bruns:** animated cursors aren't drawn.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and against the PC versions under Wine.
- Sound wasn't listened to.
- No code review was done on this release.
