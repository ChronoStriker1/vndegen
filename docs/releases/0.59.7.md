Lightning Warrior Raidy II keeps read marks and changes its music, and Eden's Ritter's CG viewer and galleries are complete.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.7-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.7.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.7-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **Ikura GDL:** Lightning Warrior Raidy II keeps read marks as the PC does, so Skip set to Read Only stops at new text, and Nonstop works.
- **Ikura GDL:** Raidy II's music changes as the story asks. Before, the first track kept playing.
- **Ikura GDL:** Raidy II's opening shows its logo over the desert.
- **Ikura GDL:** gallery unlocks and the ending's clear flag are kept when you leave through VNDegen's menu.
- **Ikura GDL:** comparisons in scripts work as on the PC, in Raidy and Raidy II.
- **NekoSDK:** the CG viewer in Eden's Ritter 1 and 1_2 has its control panel.
- **NekoSDK:** Eden's Ritter 1's galleries and scene lists include the Extra Story and side-story entries, Extra Story 1 and 2 start the right stories, and five side-story pictures now show.
- **NekoSDK:** Eden's Ritter 1_2 plays a voice line that was missing.

## Known limits

- NekoSDK: particles (command 150) and Eden's Ritter 1_2's title mist aren't drawn.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and against the PC versions under Wine.
- Raidy II's later floors weren't played by hand. All 135 of its story scripts were run in the test build, and its ending plays through to the title.
- No code review was done on this release.
