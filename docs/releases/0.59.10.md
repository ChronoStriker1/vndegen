ANIM's story text uses each game's own font and its cut-ins show, and Mesu Kyoushi's auto save keeps to slot 0, so VNDegen no longer overwrites PC saves on the first page.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.10-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.10.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.10-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **ANIM:** story text uses each game's own font, as on the PC: MS Gothic in Double Date Dilemma and MS PMincho in Magical Sword, through VNDegen's substitutes. Settings' font list works, and the chosen font is kept where the PC keeps it.
- **ANIM:** Double Date Dilemma's cut-ins, Magical Sword's skill status window and both games' memory filters now show. They had been drawn off screen. Saves made in those scenes load on the PC with them in place.
- **ANIM:** hiding a character crossfades the scene, as on the PC.
- **ANIM:** the Volume screen's Play (mute) and Allow Test buttons work, and To Settings opens Settings.
- **ANIM:** Magical Sword lets you edit a save card's comment, and Double Date Dilemma's Load screen gains its AUTO SAVE page.
- **ANIM:** the Sprites viewer's folder button opens the screenshot folder.
- **BISHOP BSX:** the auto save goes only to slot 0, shown as AUTO ONLY, and slots from 1 take your own saves, as on the PC. Before, VNDegen spread its auto saves over most of the first page and refused your own saves there, and after a sync it could overwrite saves made on the PC in those slots.

## Known limits

- **ANIM:** the goddess's eyelids, faint seams in Double Date Dilemma's second swimsuit event, the goddess close-up at about 25 fps, and approximated screen effects.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and against the PC versions under Wine.
- ANIM's Volume samples and mutes were checked by output level, not by ear.
- No code review was done on this release.
