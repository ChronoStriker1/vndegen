Text placement fixes for Yarinaoshi no Christmas and The Alchemist & His Battle-Scarred Homunculus.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.26.5-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.26.5.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- Yarinaoshi no Christmas: text is no longer drawn half again too large. The game asks for Meiryo, which Windows draws at about two thirds of its requested size, while VNDegen's stand-in filled the whole space. Size, letter spacing, line pitch and position now match a Windows screenshot of the English fan patch: line ends within a pixel, baselines on its rows, and no line runs off the screen. (#65)
- The Alchemist: text sits 3 pixels lower, as in the publisher's screenshots.

## Known limits

- Christmas's log text position is worked out from the two fonts' heights, not measured, since no screenshot shows the PC's log.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Christmas was released only in Japanese, so its reference is two screenshots of the fan translation running on Windows.
