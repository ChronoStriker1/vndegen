A new engine: EAGLS, on VNDegen's own runtime, with the English Oppai no Ouja 48.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.53.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.53.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.53.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **EAGLS**, Tech-Arts' engine, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is the English release of Oppai no Ouja 48: its title, opening movie, story with voices and music, choices and name entry.
- The game's own message window with its wheel and system menu, save and load pages, backlog, settings, Soundtrack and animation settings are drawn from its own parts, so they look as on the PC.
- Saves from the PC version, in the game's `Save` folder, load in VNDegen on the same message.
- EAGLS games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/eagls.md](../eagls.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- A slot saved in VNDegen shows in the PC game's Load screen, but loading it there crashes the PC game. The PC's own saves are unaffected and load in VNDegen.
- The title's CG View and Scene View, the locked bonus, and the window's Oppai Gallery and Oppai View buttons do nothing.
- The Champion Bra page shows every bra as not found.
- A few transition effects are drawn as crossfades.
- The settings show only their Text and Sound tabs.
- The name is typed only from a hardware keyboard; without one, the default name stays.
- Scenes and CGs seen in VNDegen don't show as seen on the PC.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- The story was played up to the heroine map. The endings and the heroine routes after the map weren't played.
- The opening and one later movie were checked for frame rate on the Mac and the iPad simulator, and every frame was shown. The other movies weren't played.
- No code review was done on this release.
