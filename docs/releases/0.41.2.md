On the Mac, the window buttons no longer sit over a game.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.41.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.41.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly. Nothing changes on iPhone and iPad in this release.

## Fixes

- Mac: the close, minimise and zoom buttons are hidden while a game plays (#90). The window has no title bar, so they used to sit over the top-left corner of a game that fills the window, such as a 1280x800 game or a Twine page. They show in the library, on a game's page, in Settings, and while the VNDegen menu is open over a game. Cmd-W, Cmd-M, the menu bar and full screen work as before.

## Known limits

- A Unity game that runs in CrossOver's window or its own Mac window keeps that window's own buttons.
- Violated Hero was seen to stop partway through its opening (#85). It hasn't been reproduced since and is still open.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or hardware controller was used.
- The Mac app was driven through its test hooks, not with real keys or clicks. Minimise and close were sent as the calls Cmd-M and Cmd-W make.
- Two games were used: an RPG Maker MZ game in the hub's window and a Ren'Py game in its own window. The other engines share the same player view and weren't run one by one.
- A game that fails to start was checked by reading the code, not by a run.
- Nothing was run on a simulator: the change is Mac only, and the iOS build passes.
- No code review was done on this release.
