A new engine: Nitroplus N2System, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Hanachirasu.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.42.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.42.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.42.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Nitroplus N2System**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is the English release of Hanachirasu: its title, story with text, voices, music and sound, choices, the opening movie, the right-click menu, backlog, Config, Hide Text, Auto, Skip and Return to Title. The game's menus are its own scripts, so they look and work as on the PC.
- N2System saves are the PC engine's own files, where the PC version keeps them. A save made on a PC loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- N2System games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/n2system.md](../n2system.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- N2System: only Hanachirasu is known to play. Its archives are encrypted, and only its key is known, so another encrypted N2System game needs its own.
- A loaded save starts its text block again from the beginning. After loading a save the PC made, the lines of that block already shown appear twice in the backlog.
- The read-text record that Skip uses isn't shared with the PC.
- Toggle Fullscreen in the game's menu does nothing; the app's own window controls set the window.
- Text uses the shared Tahoma substitute, so some lines wrap at a different word than on the PC.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Hanachirasu was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- The story was played and skipped through to chapter 5. The later chapters, the endings, the extras and most choices weren't reached.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine. Saves made at a choice or during an animation weren't tried.
- The opening movie was measured at its full 29.97 fps on the Mac app and the iPad simulator.
- The last fixes to loading and saving were checked in the Linux test build and under Wine, not again in the apps.
- No code review was done on this release.
