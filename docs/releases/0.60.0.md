A new engine: SystemAoi (SoftHouseChara), on VNDegen's own runtime, with BUNNY BLACK 3, from the prologue through the town's opening turns and expeditions into 千樹庭.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **SystemAoi**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is BUNNY BLACK 3 (SystemAoi 5), in Japanese.
- The town's commands: 建設, 内政, 休憩, 配置, 装備, 育成 and 商会.
- Units joining, battles with the game's orders and presets, and 千樹庭's 3D areas with their enemy groups, crystals and pickups.
- The town, its units, the battles and the dungeon live in the PC exe rather than the game's scripts, so they were rebuilt from the game's tables and from play of the original. The dungeon is drawn from the game's own models. [docs/systemaoi-cleanroom.md](../systemaoi-cleanroom.md) marks which rules were measured and which were inferred.
- Saves are the PC's own `Bunny3.sav` files, so they load both ways, in the town and in a dungeon.
- Movies play.
- The game starts on the Visual novel layout: the D-pad and left stick walk the dungeon, and the right stick moves the pointer for the town and battle screens.
- [docs/systemaoi.md](../systemaoi.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- This release covers the opening. Missions, the town's defence, 解任/編成, stairs between floors and the dungeons after 千樹庭 come later, since the opening doesn't reach them.
- Where enemy groups first stand and how they move is VNDegen's own approximation.
- The battle backdrop is a still of the dungeon view.
- The save comment takes no text.
- Option and Extra aren't built.
- With the touch gamepad layout, taps on the screen don't reach the game; use the Visual novel layout or the right stick's pointer for the town and battle screens.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
