Seven more NekoSDK games now have their own menus, galleries, settings and save screens, laid out as on the PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.27.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.27.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: NekoSDK games

- Funbag Fantasy 3 if, Funbag Fantasy 4, Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi, Horny Elves and a Moral Orc, Ore o Hoshigaru Tsuma to Gibo, and two more NekoSDK games play, with their own title, save, load, Config, gallery, music and log screens, each rebuilt from the PC game.
- Movies play, through the same decoder the app uses for KiriKiri games.
- Routes, endings and staff rolls work as on the PC, and each game's choices and counters follow its own rules.
- Scenes you unlock and text you read move both ways with a PC backup. Skip can pass only text you've read, starting from the PC's read marks. (#46)
- Save cards can be locked, and you can type a comment on a card, as on the PC. A PC save's comment shows too.
- Every Config option that means something here works: effects, waits, the confirm key, pausing while VNDegen isn't the active app, full screen on the Mac, the H sound effects' and background voices' volumes with their test sounds, and skipping read text.
- Text is set at each game's own size and spacing, measured against the PC. Open Sans stands in for Lucida Sans Unicode.
- Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi's sprite gallery works, and its outfit lock dresses the story's sprites in the outfit you choose.
- Ore o Hoshigaru Tsuma to Gibo's choice between detailed and normal CG art works.
- The title screens' Version info, Manual and web survey buttons work. The manual opens inside the app and offline, and the web survey asks before it opens your browser.
- Yarinaoshi no Christmas also gets its Manual and Version info buttons.

## Known limits

- Not built yet: the sparkles around the GOOD Timing count, a noise effect in some scenes of The Alchemist, and Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi's 4:3 mode. Settings for a USB toy are kept and do nothing.
- Config's hover help can sit up to 20 pixels off near the screen edge, 32 in The Alchemist.
- Some details were measured under Wine or worked out rather than watched on a PC: parts of GOOD Timing's rules, Funbag Fantasy 4's gallery tab order, and where the Funbag Fantasy games' and Christmas's logs place their text.
- Four long Japanese lines in two games run past the screen's edge, as their PC versions would.
- Nine more NekoSDK games are detected and say on their page that they can't run yet.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Each screen was compared with the PC game running under Wine on Linux, and checked in the Mac app and on the iPad simulator.
