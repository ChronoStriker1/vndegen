BUNNY BLACK 3 gets town posts, 内政's 交換 and 解任.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.4-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.4.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.4-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **SystemAoi:** BUNNY BLACK 3's town posts. From turn 20, 配置 has 一覧, 組織図 and 編成, and on 組織図 you pick a member and then one of the nine posts, from 街長 to 衛生担当. Posts are kept in saves both ways with the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** 内政's 交換, the six fixed trades, which end the turn as on the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** 解任 takes a member off the board, from the field or the reserve.
- **SystemAoi:** the town's result and income boxes draw over the dimmed town map, as on the PC.

## Known limits

- Posts don't change the town's numbers or the posted member's stats yet.
- The limits listed for 0.60.0 to 0.60.2 still apply.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The game was played in the Linux test build and the Mac app, and saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
