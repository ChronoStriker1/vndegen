BUNNY BLACK 3 gets town posts, 内政's 交換 and 解任.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.4-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.4.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.4-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Added

- **BISHOP BSX:** Mesu Kyoushi 3 and 4's free-sight meter. On the dress screen, FACE, BUST and HIP pick a fixed view of close-ups, and dragging the knob or clicking the meter sets your own. The view stays on later lines and is kept in saves, so it moves with the PC both ways.

## Added

- **SystemAoi:** BUNNY BLACK 3 reaches 炎山洞, which opens through its event on 遠征, as on the PC. Stairs lead between its areas, and door spots register new entry areas on the dungeon select screen.
- **SystemAoi:** the マクドゥの試験 missions start at turn 17, with their set-up panel, 提出 in the town, rewards and deadlines.
- **SystemAoi:** area events and the first-step explanations play when the original plays them, and so do the town's events after each turn, such as recruit scenes. Messages show the numbers they name.
- Saves with missions and 炎山洞 load both ways with the PC.

## Known limits

- A hidden door opens only while a unit with 感知 is placed. That is VNDegen's reading of the original, so 炎山洞's lower areas wait until such a unit joins, and recruiting isn't built yet.
- Which mission comes, when several could start on the same turn, is picked at random.
- The town's defence, 解任/編成, recruiting from battles and the dungeons after 炎山洞 come later.
- The limits listed for 0.60.0 still apply.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
## Added

- **SystemAoi:** BUNNY BLACK 3's new units join as the town's housing grows, shown in the PC's 部下が増えました。 box with their cards. サキュー, whose 感知 opens 炎山洞's hidden doors, joins once the town has a level 3 豪邸住居.
- **SystemAoi:** area bosses stand guard in their areas, and beating one brings its leader over.
- **SystemAoi:** building something for the first time plays the carpenters' scene.

## Changed

- **iPhone Duo:** with the Duo half open, nothing you tap sits on the fold. The VNDegen menu, Game commands and Cheats move to the trailing half (or below a fold that runs across), Settings and a game page's buttons keep to the leading half, and the passcode keypad puts its keys below the fold.
- **iPhone Duo:** the library's buttons, Back on a game page and in Settings, and a sheet's Done move into a column on the side where the system puts its bars, below the camera: on the outer display in both orientations, and on the inner display held like a book. Held upright, the inner display keeps the top bar. Buttons that don't fit go into a More menu.
- **Library:** the Grid and Header list take their columns from the space they have, with even counts where the display can fold and a wider gap at the fold. On a 13-inch iPad held upright, the grid shows six columns instead of five.
- **Game page:** the banner's height comes from the page's width, so it's a little shorter on an iPhone held upright.

## Added

- **SystemAoi:** BUNNY BLACK 3's town posts. From turn 20, 配置 has 一覧, 組織図 and 編成, and on 組織図 you pick a member and then one of the nine posts, from 街長 to 衛生担当. Posts are kept in saves both ways with the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** 内政's 交換, the six fixed trades, which end the turn as on the PC.
- **SystemAoi:** 解任 takes a member off the board, from the field or the reserve.
- **SystemAoi:** the town's result and income boxes draw over the dimmed town map, as on the PC.

## Known limits

- The limits listed for 0.60.0 to 0.60.2 still apply.
