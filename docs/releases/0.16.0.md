Shows game page screenshots in a grid with a full-screen viewer, adds game speed for Godot and Flash games, and fixes white backgrounds in RPG Maker MZ cutscenes.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.16.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.16.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: screenshots on game pages

- A game page's screenshots fill a grid that grows with the window: two columns on an iPhone in portrait and in small windows, and three beside the details card on a landscape iPad or a wide Mac window. Long lists show two rows and Show all. Large tiles load the full-size image.
- A screenshot opens full screen. Swipe, click the arrows, or press left and right to move through them, and B, Escape or the close button goes back.

## New: game speed for Godot and Flash

- The Cheats page runs Godot and Flash games at 2x or 4x. Godot uses its own speed control, so physics stays as it is at 1x.
- In Flash games, timing that uses `getTimer()` and scenes with streamed sound keep normal speed.

## Fixes

- RPG Maker MZ: cutscene clips with transparency, such as No Socks RPG's, no longer show a white background. VNDegen decodes WebM clips that carry an alpha channel itself, as the PC version shows them. A clip may show blank for a moment while its first frame decodes.
- The artwork picker downloads each thumbnail once instead of twice.
- Godot builds of different branches no longer rebuild each other's work. This only affects building VNDegen.

## Known limits

- RPG Maker MZ: alpha clips smaller than 64 pixels on a side, VP8 clips with alpha, and VP9 profiles 2 and 3 still show white.
- RPG Maker MV games aren't covered by the graphics recovery that 0.15.1 added for MZ.
