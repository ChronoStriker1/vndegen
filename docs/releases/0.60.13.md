On the iPhone Duo held sideways, Clear of the camera now knows the picture size of every engine's games.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.60.13-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.60.13.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.60.13-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.





## Changed

- **iPhone Duo:** Godot, Ren'Py, Artemis, KiriKiri, SiglusEngine, TyranoScript and Flash games now tell VNDegen their picture size. With the Duo closed and held on its side at Clear of the camera, a wide game from these engines now fits below or above the camera instead of always sitting beside it. KiriKiri and Godot games that change their size while playing are followed.

## Known limits

- Twine stories report no size, because they lay out as pages and fill either area, so they stay beside the camera's column.
- Ren'Py games on the Mac and Unity games run in their own windows and aren't affected.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical iPhone Duo was used. On the iPhone Duo simulator, a Godot game (16:9) and a KiriKiri game (4:3) were checked with the camera on each side. Artemis, SiglusEngine, Ren'Py, TyranoScript and Flash were checked by unit tests only, and a game changing its size mid-game wasn't tried.
- No code review was done on this release.
