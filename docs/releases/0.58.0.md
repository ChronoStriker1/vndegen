A new engine: PJADV (PajamasEX / Production PENCIL), on VNDegen's own runtime, with Paradise Cleaning!- Tutor X Hypnosis -.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.58.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.58.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.58.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **PJADV**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is Paradise Cleaning!- Tutor X Hypnosis -: the title, all four chapters of five stages each, the scenes with their voices, the gallery, the options and the help.
- The cleaning minigame lives in the PC exe rather than the game's scripts, so it was rebuilt from play of the original. [docs/pjadv-cleanroom.md](../pjadv-cleanroom.md) marks which rules were measured and which were inferred.
- Saves are the PC's own files, so progress made on a PC loads in VNDegen and progress made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- English by default, Japanese on a Japanese device.
- The Mouse layout is the default: touch drags directly, the stick moves the pointer, A clicks, and the shoulder buttons change tools (the PC uses the mouse wheel).
- [docs/pjadv.md](../pjadv.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- Which minigame sound plays for which event was matched by ear, and so was the timing of the clear flash.
- The original sometimes ignores a second wipe made right after the first; VNDegen counts it.
- The Window mode option changes only its label.
- Achievements aren't shown.
- The game's Chinese scenes aren't offered: they were left in from another Pencil game and ask for pictures and voices this game doesn't ship.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or physical controller was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- The original wasn't run in Japanese.
- No code review was done on this release.
