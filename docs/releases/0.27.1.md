NekoSDK saves now move to a PC with their pictures, and saves made while something is animating pick up where they left off. The Alchemist's film noise and the GOOD Timing heart are drawn, and Config's hover help sits exactly where the PC puts it.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.27.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.27.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- NekoSDK: a save made in VNDegen lost every picture when loaded in the PC game. The newer engine reads a second copy of each picture's name, which VNDegen left empty, while the older engine crashes if it's filled in. Each game now writes the form its own PC saves use, checked under Wine. Saves you made in an earlier version load correctly in VNDegen. To move one to a PC, load it in VNDegen and save it again. (#68)
- NekoSDK: a repeating animation, a flipbook or a GOOD Timing splash saved while it runs keeps going after a load, in VNDegen and in the PC game. Before, the PC played each animation once and dropped it.
- NekoSDK: every PC save tested, 166 of them across the nine games, now reads and writes back byte for byte, so nothing in a PC save is lost when VNDegen rewrites it.
- The Alchemist & His Battle-Scarred Homunculus: the film noise over its flashbacks is drawn, as the PC draws it.
- Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi: the count before GOOD Timing is the PC's heart, its twelve segments going dark one per count.
- Config's hover help in every NekoSDK game is placed exactly where the PC places it. Test Game 94's help had been 164 pixels out of place, and its sound page showed the wrong help line. Funbag Fantasy 4's auto speed setting gets the help it was missing.
- Ore o Hoshigaru Tsuma to Gibo's title buttons and two of Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi's moved 1 to 6 pixels, to where the PC draws them.

## Known limits

- Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi: its 4:3 mode isn't built yet, and its Config is missing the dark band the PC draws across the bottom.
- Some parts of GOOD Timing's rules are still worked out rather than watched on a PC.
- Six NekoSDK games, those that keep their story order in the program itself, are being built and still say on their page that they can't run yet.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. All nine NekoSDK games were played in the Mac app through a choice, a save, a load and a gallery replay. Haha Sannin to Ana Asobi and Christmas have no choices, so they were played to their third scene instead. The iPhone and iPad app was built but not played.
- Saves moving to a PC were checked with the PC games running under Wine on Linux, not on Windows.
- The Mac app was checked on one Mac only.
