Adds Godot games on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.9.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.9.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- Godot games run inside VNDegen's window. Godot 4 games run on Godot 4.7, and Godot 3 games on Godot 3.6, which covers games exported with Godot 4.2 to 4.5 and Godot 3.3 to 3.6. VNDegen loads the game's pack unchanged, from a `.pck` beside the `.exe` or from inside the `.exe`.
- Godot 4 draws through Metal on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Godot 3 draws through OpenGL on Mac and through ANGLE on iPhone and iPad. Godot needs a Mac with Apple silicon.
- Controllers reach the game as an Xbox controller, and touch and the mouse as Godot's mouse. A hardware keyboard types into the game's text fields, and the Mac keyboard and mouse wheel work.
- Saves go where Windows keeps them. `user://` is under the game's AppData stand-in, so Ludusavi backups from Windows, a Steam Deck, or Godot's Linux and Mac builds restore there. Games that save beside their `.exe` save in their own folder.
- Files outside the pack are found whatever case the game names them in.
- Games VNDegen can't run say why on their page: Godot C# games, encrypted packs, GDExtension and GDNative games, and games with Steam built in.

## Known limits

- A focused text field doesn't open the on-screen keyboard, and held keys don't repeat.
- `OS.alert()` writes to the log instead of showing a dialog.
- If Godot fails partway through starting a game, for example because its renderer doesn't start, it runs no more games until VNDegen is reopened.
- Godot 4 games written for the compatibility renderer run on the mobile renderer on Metal.
- Tested with the 4 Godot games in the library that VNDegen supports. All reach their title, save and load on the Mac and the iPad simulator, and one also on the iPhone simulator. `docs/godot.md` has the details.
