Wolf RPG Editor 3.5 games now run, with saves that move to and from the PC. RPG Maker VX Ace games play their movies, and RPG Maker games show their message boxes in the app.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.19.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.19.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New: Wolf RPG Editor 3.5

- Wolf RPG Editor 3.5 games run, such as The Lewd Adventure of the Clumsy Witch Seris. VNDegen reads their compressed data.
- Their saves move between VNDegen and the PC in both directions. After a save moves, the HUD, minimap and labels come back once the menu has been opened and closed.
- Archives from Wolf RPG Editor 3 now open. Two Beasts Or Not To Beast! starts and stops at its first mouse prompt, until mouse input arrives.
- ANGEL QUEST Holy St. Azuria plays, with its opening picture.

## New: RPG Maker movies and message boxes

- RPG Maker VX Ace games play their movies, centred at their own size as on the PC.
- RPG Maker XP, VX and VX Ace games show their message boxes in the app and wait until you press OK, as on the PC.

## Fixes

- Wolf RPG Editor: more accurate menus, pictures and timing. A quick tap in a menu is no longer missed, common events with text arguments return their values to the right place, the screen colour no longer tints pictures, and descenders in text no longer clip.

## Licensing correction

- Releases 0.12.0 to 0.17.0 also contained an unused copy of the old mkxp-z framework (GPL-3.0), left over in the folder they were built in, so they are GPL-3.0 as a whole. It was never used to run games. 0.18.0 and later don't contain it.

## Known limits

- Wolf RPG Editor: The Clumsy Witch Seris has no minimap, some building labels sit on the wrong buildings, and its system voices are silent. These are in progress. Wolf RPG Editor Pro's encrypted data and DxLib archive version 7 aren't read, and no library game needs them.
- RPG Maker: a long message box can't scroll through the iPad touch mouse's pad. Movies with Vorbis sound play silent. The one library movie with it is silent anyway.
- The Mac app wasn't tested for these features, because the test Macs were locked. The iPad simulator was.
