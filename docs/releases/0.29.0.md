Funbag Fantasy 2 plays, from New Game to all six endings, with saves that move to and from a PC.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.29.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.29.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## New

- NekoSDK: Funbag Fantasy 2 plays. It's the third game that keeps its story order in the program itself, so VNDegen plays it from a table of that order traced from the PC game. The story follows the PC's scene order through all six endings.
- Its own menus: the title with its three turning lace circles, Start offering Prologue or Main Story once the prologue is read, and Extra with the CG, Scene and Music galleries.
- 100 save slots across ten pages, numbered 001 to 100, with no quick save, as on the PC. A PC save loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- The message window's four round buttons always show, and the control panel comes up when the pointer reaches the bottom. On a touch screen the first touch brings the panel up and the next presses a button.
- The in-window log, stepped with the window's back and forward buttons, the choice box, and the question boxes, each with its own plate.
- Its effects: glowing dots that rise and sway, drifting fog, and the sword strokes painted over the scene. Config's Effects switch turns the dots off.
- Where the window is nearer 4:3 than 16:9, as on an iPad, the picture stays on top and the message window moves under it.

## Fixes

- NekoSDK, every newer game: a flipbook animation saved while it showed its fifth picture was read back as film noise. It now comes back as the animation.
- NekoSDK: a scrolling picture keeps its speed through a load and a save, so a PC save that holds one comes through unchanged.

## Known limits

- Funbag Fantasy 2's message text is set in Open Sans, sized to the publisher's Windows screenshots. The PC asks for Lucida Sans Unicode, so line ends can fall a few pixels differently.
- Its Config's colour depth, resolution and "ask for the resolution at start" rows are drawn disabled, since VNDegen sizes the window itself.
- Its scripts name one sound the game doesn't ship. Nothing plays there, on the PC too.
- One of its scenes has no line to save on, so its place in a PC save is inferred from the scenes around it.
- How many glowing dots an effect makes is fitted to the one scene measured, about a hundred.
- Three NekoSDK games are still being built and say on their page that they can't run yet: Mankitsu Happening, Yabai! -Fukushuu Yami Site- and Funbag Fantasy: Sideboob Story.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Funbag Fantasy 2 was played to all six endings in the Linux test build, in the Mac app, and on the iPad simulator by touch.
- Saves moving to and from a PC were checked with the PC game running under Wine on Linux, not on Windows.
- The Mac app was checked on one Mac only.
- The last small change to the shared code, to how film noise is told from a picture in the same slot, was tested but not read by a reviewer.
