Fixes for NScripter games.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.8.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.8.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixed

- NScripter transitions from the `breakup.dll`, `lngtwave.dll` and `trvswave.dll` effect plugins look as they do on Windows. Characters break apart and fly off, and screens ripple, instead of crossfading.
- MP3 music and voices play in NScripter games.
- NScripter's text input and yes/no boxes look and sit as they do on Windows, and input boxes take the size the game asks for.
- NScripter games keep their global variables, such as the save dates a game's own save screen shows, when VNDegen is closed or the system ends it in the background.
- NScripter saves made inside a game's subroutines load correctly, and stay byte-identical to the PC version's.
- NScripter sound is decoded on its own thread, so a slow decode can't cause a dropout.
- NScripter backgrounds no longer show black holes where a color matched the image's top-left pixel.

## Known limits

- Other NScripter effect plugins still crossfade. CD audio, MIDI music, Lua scripts and two rarely used archive compression types aren't supported, because no tested game uses them.
- Tested with the 6 NScripter games in the library on the Mac and the iPad simulator. All reach their title, play, save and load, and movies and dungeon walking hold 60 fps.
