A new engine: BISHOP BSX, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Mesu Kyoushi ~Injoku no Kyoushitsu~ Immoral Edition.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.55.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.55.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.55.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **BISHOP BSX**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is Mesu Kyoushi ~Injoku no Kyoushitsu~ Immoral Edition, in its English translation: the title, the school week with its map and target cards, events and choices, eyecatches, voices and music, through to the endings.
- The PC engine's screens are drawn from the game's own layout: message window, menu bar, Save and Load, backlog, settings, the item screen, and STRIP and VIEW.
- BSX saves are the PC engine's own files in the game's save folder. A save made on a PC loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC, with read text and choices kept.
- BSX games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/bsx.md](../bsx.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- The museum (CG, music, scene and bust-up viewer) isn't there yet; its title button does nothing.
- Settings are VNDegen's own, kept in `save/vndegen.cfg`, not the PC's. Full screen, effect detail and auto-select do nothing.
- Cut-ins and the voice repeat modes aren't there.
- VIEW uses three fixed positions, without the free-sight meter.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- A skip-play reached an ending and the staff roll in the Linux test build. The Mac app and the iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and loading it after a restart.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- Sound wasn't listened to on the Mac or iPad.
- No code review was done on this release.
