ANIM's Extra galleries for both of its games, and Magical Sword's autosaves.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.2-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **ANIM:** the Extra screen's CG, Scenes and Music pages in Double Date Dilemma and Magical Sword. The CG page has its full-screen viewer, scenes replay and return to the gallery, and the music room plays tracks in the PC's order with Stop and Repeat. What is unlocked is kept in the game's own system file, so it is shared with the PC version both ways.
- **ANIM:** Magical Sword saves automatically before each choice, as on the PC, and its Load screen has the AUTO SAVE page. Its autosaves load on the PC too.

## Fixed

- **ANIM:** choices with one or two options sit where the PC puts them.

## Known limits

- The Extra screen's Animation and Sprites pages aren't built yet; their buttons do nothing.
- In Double Date Dilemma's Scenes page, the blurb text is slightly narrower than the PC's.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The galleries were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch, and against the PC version under Wine.
- No code review was done on this release.
