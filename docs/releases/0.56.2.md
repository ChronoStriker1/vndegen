Fixes found while adding 27 new test games: import, Wolf RPG, KiriKiri and YU-RIS, with four more games that now play.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.56.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.56.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.56.2-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixes

- Import: a game packed inside two wrapper folders unpacks. A GOG installer inside an archive keeps the game's own name, so its details are found. A GOG game whose files sit at the top level no longer offers to extract its web cache as if it were the game.
- Wolf RPG: events that run automatically repeat while their page is active, as on the PC. The Succubus of the Forest and a Doctor plays past its opening, and Two Beasts starts a new game from the keyboard. A move route on a hidden event ends at once, so President Yukino goes on past its prologue.
- KiriKiri: Tenioha! feat. Mami starts. A transition the engine lacks plays as a crossfade, without a warning box.
- YU-RIS: Holy Maid Academy, on engine version 0.491, plays.

## Known limits

- Tenioha's save menu appears only when the mouse hovers over it, so it can't be opened by touch yet.
- In Life as a Caretaker, two dialogs draw some kanji as empty boxes on the Mac. The iPad draws them.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. Each fix was checked in the Mac app, and the Wolf, Godot, YU-RIS and KiriKiri games also on an iPad simulator.
- The Wolf, KiriKiri and YU-RIS changes were compared before and after on the other test games of each engine, with no differences other than the fixed games.
- No code review was done on this release.
