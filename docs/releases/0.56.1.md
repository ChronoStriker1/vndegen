The fourth BISHOP BSX game: Mesu Kyoushi 3 ~Inetsu no Gakusha~ Immoral Edition.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.56.1-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.56.1.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.56.1-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- Mesu Kyoushi 3 ~Inetsu no Gakusha~ Immoral Edition, in its English machine translation, runs on the BSX runtime: the opening movie, the school map and the search map, events and choices, achievements and their DATA screen, the FACE, BUST and HIP views, the backlog and settings.
- Save and Load use the PC's own files, so saves move between VNDegen and a PC copy both ways. Achievements unlocked in VNDegen show as unlocked on the PC.

## Fixes

- In all four Mesu Kyoushi games, save slots are numbered from 0, as the PC shows them.

## Known limits

- The museum isn't there yet, so its two completion achievements can't be earned.
- The STRIP button (the dress screen) does nothing.
- The settings screen lacks BGM SELECT, the per-character voice details and the BGV volume.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used, and no iPhone simulator was used for this game. A play reached an ending and the title in the Linux test build. The Mac app and an iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and loading it after a restart, and the iPad also with every file name's case swapped.
- The opening movie showed all of its frames on the Mac and the iPad simulator.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
