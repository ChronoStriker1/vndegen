Princess Colette's Great Adventure plays through its post-game, so both Smile Game Builder games play to their very end.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.61.10-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.61.10.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.61.10-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Changed

- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure** plays through its post-game: the North Tower's five laps, the new scene its fourth lap opens in the castle basement, and ドラコ with her two battles and her scene, to the closing message. Saves move both ways with the PC version there.

## Fixed

- **Smile Game Builder:** leaving the North Tower by a floor's gate keeps the lap, as on the PC; it used to send the party back to the first lap and the castle.
- **Smile Game Builder:** battle and menu skill lists show the skills the hero has learned, as on the PC; メイリ and サキュバス no longer offer their level 1 skills.
- **Smile Game Builder:** a message that ends with a blank line after a full window shows that empty page, as on the PC.
- **Smile Game Builder:** a hero who falls in battle lies down, dimmed, as on the PC.

## Known limits

- **Princess Colette's Great Adventure:** how often a status ailment lands is a guess; マンドラゴラ's パニックボイス does less damage than on the PC, and the PC clears confusion at the end of the turn. After a message, the PC keeps an empty message box under the next choice; VNDegen doesn't.

## What wasn't tested

- Real iPhones and iPads.
- The North Tower's lap beds on 9F, 11F and 12F.
