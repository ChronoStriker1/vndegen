Wolf RPG Editor saves keep more of the screen: The Clumsy Witch Seris's HUD and President Yukino's camera.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.59.6-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.59.6.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.59.6-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## Fixed

- **Wolf RPG Editor:** The Clumsy Witch Seris keeps its HUD, minimap and portrait when you load a save, whether the save was made on the PC or in VNDegen.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** President Yukino's PC version opens VNDegen saves with the camera where it was, instead of at the map's corner.
- **Wolf RPG Editor:** Drain Dungeon's progress counter is kept in saves.

## Known limits

- Wolf RPG Editor pictures with effects, XY or cut images, or other text codes still aren't saved. No library game has one on screen at a save point. Seris's window pictures aren't saved.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. These were checked in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, and against the PC versions under Wine.
- A VNDegen save wasn't made on the iPad simulator, and Drain Dungeon wasn't saved through its own menu.
- No code review was done on this release.
