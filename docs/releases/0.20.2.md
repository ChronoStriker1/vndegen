Fixes the hero not showing in new games in several Wolf RPG Editor games.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.20.2-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.20.2.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.

## Fixes

- Wolf RPG Editor: a new game now shows the hero in ANGEL QUEST and MARAGON QUEST, and in other games that set the party's graphics the same way, such as Parade Buster, YUME-SAME and Holy Grail. Before, the hero was invisible.

## Known limits

- Wolf RPG Editor: a save made in VNDegen while the hero was invisible stays that way until the game sets the party's graphics again.
