A new engine: Bruns, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Orc Kingdom: Monmusu Breeder King.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.56.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.56.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.56.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Bruns**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is Orc Kingdom: Monmusu Breeder King, in Japanese: the title, the story, the strategy screens, the world map, dungeons with their battles and events, and village hunting.
- The game's own Save, Load, settings and backlog screens work, as do choices, the right-side menu bar, voices and music.
- Bruns saves are the PC engine's own files, so a save made on a PC loads in VNDegen and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC.
- Bruns games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/bruns.md](../bruns.md) has the details.

## Fixes

- The license notices for the stb and dr_wav libraries used by the EAGLS and ExHibit runtimes are now included in the app. Releases 0.53.0 to 0.55.4 left them out.

## Known limits

- Flow mode isn't there; its menu bar slot stays empty.
- The license dialog shown on the PC's first start is accepted without being shown.
- The save comment box keeps the default comment.
- Animated cursors and mask pictures aren't drawn, and the window size and full screen settings do nothing.
- The backlog's spacing is close to the PC's but not the same, and the page a load restores isn't in the backlog.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- The sim side was compared screen by screen with the original under Wine, and a 3,000-click random run found no errors or hangs. The Mac app and the iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and loading it after a restart.
- Once, on the iPad simulator, a load showed a line a few pages past the save; a repeat loaded the right line, and it couldn't be reproduced.
- Saves were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- No code review was done on this release.
