A new engine: IPAC, on VNDegen's own runtime, with Mamono Musume to no Seikatsu: Lamia no Baai.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.51.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.51.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.51.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **IPAC**, on VNDegen's own runtime, written in C and MIT licensed, on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first game is Mamono Musume to no Seikatsu: Lamia no Baai (Japanese): its title, story, choices, voices, music and sound, and every route to its ending.
- The game's own save and load pages, configuration, backlog, CG gallery with its variant viewer and scene recall are drawn from its own parts, so they look as on the PC.
- IPAC saves and settings are the PC engine's own files, kept where the PC keeps them. A save made on a PC loads in VNDegen, and a save made in VNDegen loads on the PC. Gallery progress moves both ways too.
- IPAC games start on the "Visual novel" controller layout.
- [docs/ipac.md](../ipac.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- The configuration's font page is drawn, but its settings do nothing; text always uses the same font.
- Read marks aren't kept, so Skip skips all text, as the PC does by default.
- "Return to title" asks with VNDegen's own dialog, since the PC uses a Windows message box.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device was used. The game was played in the Linux test build, the Mac app and an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, and also with every file name's case swapped.
- Routes were played to their endings in the Linux test build. The Mac app and the iPad simulator were checked from the title through a save and a load.
- Saves and settings were exchanged with the original engine under Wine both ways.
- Sound wasn't listened to on the Mac or iPad.
- No code review was done on this release.
