A new engine: Construct 3, in the web view, with Transpile Girl Rescue Operation!.

## Downloads

- `VNDegen-0.45.0-macOS.zip` for Mac (macOS 15 or later). It is ad-hoc signed: right-click VNDegen and choose Open the first time, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VNDegen.app`.
- `VNDegen-0.45.0.ipa` for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later). Update it from the AltStore or SideStore source (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/main/altstore/source.json`), or sideload it with Sideloadly.
- `VNDegen-0.45.0-LGPL-sources.tar`: the exact source of the LGPL libraries in this release. [docs/relinking.md](https://github.com/ChronoStriker1/vndegen/blob/main/docs/relinking.md) says how to replace one.

## New

- **Construct 3** games run in VNDegen's web view on Mac, iPhone and iPad. The first is Transpile Girl Rescue Operation!, in its Windows WebView2 build: title, story with voices, Options, the minigames, saving and loading.
- Sounds load the first time each one plays, where the PC build loads all of them before its title. The game opens in about 2 to 3 seconds instead of about 20, and uses under a third of the memory.
- Construct games start on "Visual novel for Construct": A clicks, the D-pad and left stick give the arrow keys, and the right stick moves the pointer. Touch works as the mouse.
- The Windows wrapper files the game doesn't need in VNDegen can be removed from the game's page.
- [docs/construct.md](../construct.md) has the details.

## Known limits

- Construct saves stay in VNDegen and move between your devices with VNDegen's save sync, not to a PC, where the game keeps them inside the browser's own database. Twine games work the same way.
- Steam features do nothing.
- On iPhone and iPad in Touch or Automatic mode, VNDegen's menu button covers most of the game's gear button; tap its left edge, or show the touch gamepad, which moves the menu button (#183).
- Pressing or tapping very fast through a scene change can freeze the story on that line. The PC build does the same.
- Construct's HTML5 and NW.js exports are recognised but weren't played.

## What wasn't tested

- No physical device or iPhone simulator was used. The game was played in the Mac app and on an iPad simulator, by touch and with the touch gamepad, also with every file name's case swapped.
- Frame rate was 60 fps on the Mac app and the iPad simulator, in the menus and a minigame.
- No code review was done on this release.
