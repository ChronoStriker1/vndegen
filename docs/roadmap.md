## Licenses
VNDegen should stay free to distribute and may be sold on the App Store later. Releases up to 0.11.0 include mkxp-z, the first RGSS runtime, so they are GPL-3.0 as a whole, as the README's License section explains. From 0.12.0 VNDegen's own RGSS runtime replaces it. Releases 0.12.0 to 0.17.0 still carried an unused copy of mkxp-z's framework, left over in the folder they were built in, so they are GPL-3.0 as a whole too. From 0.18.0 no GPL component is left:
- VNDegen's own MIT runtimes replace the GPL ones. The clean-room RGSS runtime replaced mkxp-z in 0.12.0. NScripter and AliceSoft System 3 and System 4 are clean-room from the start, so VNDegen never ships ONScripter, xsystem35, or xsystem4. The clean-room runtimes are written from the game files and the engines' own documentation, never from GPL code. The original engines run under Wine only to settle open questions, such as the PC save format. With no GPL runtime left, the app is MIT for its own code plus permissive notices.
- New engines use only permissive licenses: MIT, BSD, zlib, Apache-2.0, ISC, MPL-2.0, or public domain, and OFL-1.1 for fonts. One exception, agreed with the maintainer: NekoSDK's WMV movies use the LGPL FFmpeg the app already ships for KiriKiri, because Apple doesn't decode WMV and no permissive decoder is known. The KiriKiri framework exports a small decoder over it, `vnffmovie`, which the NekoSDK runtime finds at run time, so the app links FFmpeg once ([video.md](video.md#vnffmovie-wmv-through-the-apps-ffmpeg)).
- The LGPL libraries linked statically today are item 1 under Next.

## Set aside
- Playing over AirPlay with the iPhone or iPad as the controller (#66), set aside by the maintainer on 2026-11-10. iOS gives an app a TV to draw a live game on only once the player has turned on Screen Mirroring or plugged in a cable. An AirPlay button inside the app carries sound and video files, not a live picture, and sending the game as live video would put seconds between a press and the picture. The maintainer wanted it to start from inside the app, so it stopped there. What was built worked on the simulator with Screen Mirroring or a cable: the game alone went to the TV and the device showed the controls. It was never merged, and its branch was removed at the maintainer's request.

## Finished from the plan
The items of the 2026-10-25 plan that are done, kept for their decisions and notes. The Shipped list above has the releases.
- A frame rate check of every engine plugin, limited to the games where it matters: games that aren't strictly visual novels, such as RPG Maker games, and visual novels with video, such as movies or animated clips. Text, still images and transitions don't need it. Each of those test games should reach the frame rate it targets, such as 60 fps for RPG Maker MV and MZ, and hold it on average through normal play, including scenes with heavy effects. Where a game falls short, find out whether VNDegen or the engine plugin causes it, or something on the test machine, and fix what is VNDegen's. Every new engine plugin gets the same check, on the same kinds of games, before it ships. Done on 2026-11-03, and [frame-rate.md](frame-rate.md) has the results. Its fixes shipped in 0.22.2.
- NScripter, on VNDegen's own clean-room runtime. Shipped in 0.8.0.
- A controller layout library, like Steam's controller layouts. Each game uses a layout from the library, and the player can pick another, edit it, or save their own. The library starts with VNDegen's built-in layouts, among them "Keyboard and mouse", copied from Steam's template of that name, which engines without gamepad support use by default unless an engine has a better standard layout. Shipped in 0.23.0: built-in layouts, picking one for a game, editing, and saving your own. Suggested layouts followed in 0.34.0 for RPG Maker MV and MZ and Godot, and in 0.36.0 for Artemis and Wolf RPG Editor, where a suggestion in "Visual novel" may replace only View, L3, R3 and LT (agreed with the maintainer on 2026-11-11).

- Artemis, on art3m1s-core (MPL-2.0). Shipped in 0.10.0.
- Wolf RPG Editor, on VNDegen's own runtime. Shipped in 0.11.0.
- Flash, through Ruffle (MIT or Apache-2.0) in the web view the RPG Maker MV/MZ and TyranoScript plugins already use. 14 library games. Shipped in 0.7.0.
- Godot, on Godot 3.6.3 for 3.x games and 4.7.2 for 4.x games. Shipped in 0.9.0.

A feasibility test (`docs/nekosdk.md`, with its tools under the tag `archive/ws-nekosdk`) found that every game's archives and story scripts parse, but each game's title, save, config and gallery screens, and in 6 games the story order, are compiled into its exe. It estimated 10 to 14 weeks for the 10 games whose story order is data, with VNDegen-style menus. 16 library games, 45 GB.
- Unity. **Decided 2026-11-05 (option 1, with option 2's proof):** VNDegen recognizes Unity games and says on their page why it can't run them, naming the Unity version, Mono or IL2CPP, and a Mac build the game ships. A Mac hand-off to the player's CrossOver (option 2) had its half-day proof: four games reached their titles in a new CrossOver bottle, and it would take about a week, with Windows fonts in the bottle the one fix every game needed. The coordinator, given the choice by the maintainer, had it built: on a Mac, Play opens a game's own Mac version or runs it in the player's CrossOver, in a bottle VNDegen keeps apart (0.25.0). iPhone and iPad keep the explanation. A Unity engine of VNDegen's own (options 3 and 4) isn't being done. 123 library folders: 95 Mono, 23 IL2CPP, and 3 unread installers, with 2 left out. [unity.md](unity.md) has the study, the proof, and the options.
- Cleaning up a game's engine files. After a game is added, VNDegen offers to remove the bundled Windows engine files the game no longer needs to play in VNDegen, such as NW.js or Electron runtimes, Ren'Py's Windows and Linux libraries, and engine `.exe` and `.dll` files. Each engine plugin lists exactly which files it may remove. VNDegen shows the files and the space saved, and removes them only after the user confirms. Saves and the game's own data are never touched. Shipped in 0.22.0 for RPG Maker MV and MZ, TyranoScript, and Ren'Py. The other engines list nothing.

## Runtime packages
When a game needs a runtime package that VNDegen doesn't bundle, such as the RPG Maker RTP, the app offers to download it from its official source and install it, in addition to importing it from a folder. The `RuntimePackages` module does this: an engine describes each package with its official URL and pinned SHA-256 (`RuntimePackage` in VNCore), and the module downloads, checks, and unpacks it, with an Inno Setup reader for installers like the RPG Maker RTPs'. The RPG Maker 2000 and 2003 RTPs can use it the same way.

## Out of scope for now
- 3D for AliceSoft System 4 games (SealEngine). Evenicle sets up its 3D field before its title, so it can't run, and Rance IX stops at its first battle or map. It is a whole 3D engine, 330 functions, and Evenicle needs about 300 more PartsEngine functions and most of its sound and story libraries besides. The maintainer left the call to the coordinator on 2026-11-11, who put it here: it is the largest single piece of work on the list and serves two games. The instructions Evenicle stopped on (#84) run, and its game page says it can't run yet. [system4.md](system4.md) has what is missing.
- Apple TV. When it comes, the library gets a TV layout modeled on Steam Deck's home screen: the focused game's hero art fills the screen with one row of capsules along the top or the bottom edge, and swiping up on the hero art opens a card view of every game. It joins the iPhone and iPad layouts in `LibraryLayout` and is the TV's default.
